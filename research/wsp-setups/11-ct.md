# CT dataset and setup scan (WSP radiology study)

**Scope/date rule.** CT shard: lung/chest, abdomen and cancer, coronary/cardiac CT, CBCT/low-dose and reconstruction, CT segmentation and foundation-model evaluation. The cutoff is **first public availability of the data itself on or after 2026-07-01** (not paper date). “Post-cutoff” below means the source documents a post-cutoff public dataset/file release or a newly released extension; data from older scans can qualify only as a newly released annotation/version. Status is explicit where the exact *first file availability* cannot be established.

## Summary

There are several decision-useful CT releases after the cutoff: the large longitudinal multimodal CancerVerse resource, multicenter raw-projection COBRA2026, the phantom CBCT-IQ benchmark, ImageCAS-X’s coronary re-annotation of old CCTA, TotalSegmentator v3’s new pediatric/quality-improved release, an abdominal longitudinal liver-metastasis collection, and a trabecular-bone annotation extension. These enable clinically recognizable distribution-shift, temporal-maintenance, new-structure, and new-hardware tests; they are not all equivalent in quality or clinical realism. The most important cautions are small cohorts/restricted access, reused underlying scans, version drift, and no mature hidden test set for most resources.

## Public data first released on/after 2026-07-01

### 1. CancerVerse (v3.0 public; v3.5/3.5.1 additions)
- **Name/links:** [Official Hugging Face dataset & release history](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BodyMaps/CancerVerse); [MICCAI 2026 paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/0562_paper.pdf).
- **Release/date evidence:** The dataset’s official HF page says v3.0 was the **first public version in July 2026**; v3.5 added scans/patients/annotations in September, and v3.5.1 corrected time offsets in October. The older v1 (2025) and v2 (February 2026) were explicitly “internal,” not publicly released. Thus the eligible release is public v3.0 (July; day not stated on the page), with later post-cutoff snapshots. The paper predates or describes a smaller snapshot: current page reports 24,422 scans/14,599 patients; the paper’s earlier count is 24,168/13,863. **Use and freeze a dated snapshot.**
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** Volumetric CT, chest/abdomen/pelvis; voxel-wise tumor masks for 13 cancer types, with patient-level cancer confirmation, reports, pathology and clinical variables. Supports lesion detection/localization/segmentation and longitudinal/multimodal screening.
- **Size:** Current HF snapshot: **24,422 scans, 14,599 patients**; 12,529 scans with tumor annotation; 8,799 verified healthy patients. Each cancer patient has 1–32 CT scans. CT volumes are large (download instructions indicate ~3 TB; annotations ~40 GB). Images per patient/timepoint are not a separate count.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Multiple hospitals in Switzerland and Turkey (exact site count not given); acquisition years 2010–2025. Scanner-vendor count is not specified. The paper says per-scan acquisition parameters (e.g. slice thickness, pixel spacing, reconstruction kernel) are released when available; reports, clinical variables and demographics are linked, with availability matrix. Site/scanner completeness is not guaranteed.
- **Temporal metadata:** Per-patient time-ordered studies, index date, prior scans; median 3 scans/299 days among followed-up patients, maximum follow-up up to 14.4 years on current page. Paper reports median 3 scans/299 days. Date/timing correction was made in v3.5.1; freeze version to avoid temporal-label changes.
- **Access/license:** HF public download for academic/research use; **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**, with commercial use by permission. The no-derivatives/noncommercial terms need legal review before training/redistributing derived models or dataset transformations.
- **Test-set situation:** Paper reports a patient-level train/test split (11,091/2,772 patients); the resource is openly released with tumor masks, so treat the test labels as **released**, not sealed. The paper also evaluates external cohorts, but their full availability is not established by the resource page.
- **Overlap risk:** Paper says scans come from Swiss/Turkish hospitals; no direct overlap with an older public CT benchmark is identified. Source datasets and patient-level overlap are not exhaustively audited in the cited page. Run image/metadata deduplication and check all version changes before benchmark use.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S1/S7/S8/S9/S22/S24/S25/S39/S55/S56.** Start from a locked CT-FM/nnU-Net/TotalSegmentator-like checkpoint trained on older public tumor/organ labels; move to CancerVerse held-out sites, low-prevalence screening, new cancer types, or earlier prediagnosis scans. Give both arms the same 0/5/10/25/50 target patients and unlabeled target pool; G can edit probe/SAE-localized tumor or time-change features, add a sparse new class circuit, or bake temporal feature differences. This is especially valuable for low-FP screening and tumor types with little public voxel annotation.

### 2. COBRA2026: raw radiotherapy CBCT projection benchmark
- **Name/links:** [Zenodo dataset (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21322350)](https://zenodo.org/records/21322350); [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20037); [COBRA challenge](https://cobra2026.grand-challenge.org/).
- **Release/date evidence:** Zenodo v1.0.0 gives **13 July 2026** as publication; the dataset page separately lists training-set release on the same day. This confirms data availability after cutoff. The validation and test download links list 2032 release dates, so they were not publicly downloadable as of the brief date.
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** Pelvic radiotherapy cone-beam CT with measured projections, geometry/calibration, clinical and independently reconstructed CBCT, registered planning CT, simulated matched projections, masks/FOV. Primary task is reconstruction/image correction, not diagnostic labels or organ segmentation.
- **Size:** **867 cases** (692 train, 52 validation, 123 test), ~950 GB in total; train ~750 GB. It does not state unique patient count separately; case count is the reported denominator.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Six European centers in Germany (Munich, Cologne), Netherlands (Utrecht), France (Lyon), Austria (Graz, Vienna); two CBCT system vendors (**Elekta and Varian**). The corresponding planning CT sources include Philips, Siemens, Toshiba and Canon systems. Per-case center IDs and acquisition/reconstruction metadata are supplied (YAML/XML and vendor files); age/sex metadata varies by center, with some missing cells.
- **Temporal metadata:** One radiotherapy CBCT/planning-CT pair per case; no longitudinal treatment timeline stated. Acquisition/protocol parameters are supplied.
- **Access/license:** Training data is downloadable without registration from an institutional repository; **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Data are extremely large and some vendor raw acquisition/calibration formats require the provided tooling.
- **Test-set situation:** Training set available. Validation/test are listed for release in 2032; therefore **effectively hidden/unavailable** in 2026. Challenge runs on a subset of files, not necessarily the whole raw record.
- **Overlap risk:** Newly collected routine radiotherapy cases; no known overlap with a named pre-cutoff benchmark. The registered planning CT and measured CBCT are paired but not exact ground truth; planning anatomy has been deformed and quality-reviewed. Avoid treating it as a perfect paired diagnostic CT counterfactual.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S5/S8/S12/S15/S40/S49.** Start with CT reconstruction/denoising or CT segmentation weights and transfer to Elekta/Varian pelvic CBCT. At R1/R2 use only unlabeled CBCT or a small number of reviewed cases. Compare standard fine-tuning/LoRA/physics-aware reconstruction against G’s probe-guided stem/kernel edits or interpretable correction of scanner-specific directions. Metrics: reconstruction error/SSIM on simulated matched projections, HU accuracy, paired consistency, and downstream organ Dice on a separately curated subset. This is the best shard dataset for new-hardware and reconstruction-domain shift, but 24-GB training should be patch/2D or projection-batch based.

### 3. CBCT-IQ: phantom image-quality benchmark
- **Name/links:** [Zenodo record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20124496)](https://zenodo.org/records/20124496); [arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29253).
- **Release/date evidence:** Zenodo states **29 July 2026**, version 1.0, for the files themselves (not just the 31 July preprint).
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** 2D CBCT slices from thorax and pelvis anthropomorphic phantoms; subjective four-level overall and ROI image-quality scores from three experts; regression/ordinal classification and image-quality metric benchmarking. Not patient CT.
- **Size:** 36 3D CBCT volumes and 1,764 annotated slices; 826 MB including reference/degraded images and CSV annotations. No patients.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** One Siemens ARTIS pheno C-arm CBCT system at a research hybrid operating theatre; two phantoms. Tube voltages 60/90/125 kV, pulse width 3.2/5/6.4/8 ms, three reconstruction types. No multisite/vendor or patient demographic metadata.
- **Temporal metadata:** None beyond acquisition settings; paired reference/degraded images are tied to systematic protocol changes.
- **Access/license:** Zenodo open download; **CC BY 4.0**.
- **Test-set situation:** Expert ratings and benchmark files are released; no sealed challenge test set described. Split/dedup protocol is not documented as a hidden holdout.
- **Overlap risk:** No human-patient cohort. Same phantoms/system and related repeated parameter combinations create strong within-source dependence; split by volume/acquisition, not slice.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S12/S40/S49** as a low-cost mechanistic/positive-control study: source a reconstruction/quality model on reference settings and target different kV/pulse/reconstruction settings. F gets IQA metric training/adapters; G adds interpretability-guided intensity/reconstruction-feature edits. Score ordinal agreement or rank correlation with expert MOS. A 24-GB GPU is ample (2D slices), but external clinical relevance is limited.

### 4. ImageCAS-X: coronary CCTA lumen and tree labels (new annotations on old images)
- **Name/links:** [Zenodo release (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21887809)](https://zenodo.org/records/21887809); [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30404); [project/benchmark](https://kitbransby.github.io/ImageCAS-X/).
- **Release/date evidence:** Zenodo says **11 August 2026** (v1) for the annotations and weights. The CT images are not redistributed; only new labels/geometry are post-cutoff. This is an eligible extension, not newly acquired CT.
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** CCTA, coronary lumen voxel masks, 18-segment tree labels, centerlines and mesh surfaces; segmentation/topology and scan-level disease, dominance, image-quality descriptors.
- **Size:** 800 CCTA scans from the original 1,000-case ImageCAS cohort; 200 excluded for non-diagnostic artifacts. 560/80/160 train/validation/test; test cases reannotated independently. The source describes one scan per patient; dataset is 800 patient cases.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Guangdong Provincial People’s Hospital, China; Siemens 128-slice dual-source scanner. One center/vendor. Age/sex and scan-level disease/dominance/image-quality descriptors are described, but original hospital metadata was removed. One cardiac phase per scan, selected as 30–40% or 60–70% based on image quality.
- **Temporal metadata:** No patient follow-up; cardiac-phase selection is available, not longitudinal timing.
- **Access/license:** Labels/centerlines/meshes and pretrained weights open on Zenodo, **CC BY 4.0**. Original images must be separately obtained from ImageCAS/Kaggle; its distinct terms/license apply (paper describes Apache 2.0). Ensure both licenses are compatible for the experiment.
- **Test-set situation:** 160-case test split and reference labels are included/released; not a hidden challenge set.
- **Overlap risk:** **Complete direct image overlap** with the older ImageCAS cohort (800 selected from its 1,000 scans). The key new contribution is higher-quality coronary labels and independent reannotation, not independent patient data. Do not use old ImageCAS labels or any published benchmark evaluation for a sealed test comparison.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S24/S25/S32/S56**, and low-k coronary anatomy experiments. Start from a frozen general CT encoder/TotalSegmentator/MedSAM2-like model or an ImageCAS classifier; target coronary-tree segmentation and artery-segment output. F trains a small decoder/LoRA on k patient scans; G uses vessel-probe/SAE features to add a sparse coronary branch/segment head while preserving prior anatomy outputs. Metrics: lumen Dice, clDice, centerline distance, branch-wise recall and topology error. 24 GB is feasible with cropped 3D vessel patches; full-volume 3D training at 0.5-mm spacing is memory intensive.

### 5. TotalSegmentator dataset v3 (new pediatric scans and improved labels)
- **Name/links:** [Zenodo dataset v3 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22688904)](https://zenodo.org/records/22688904); [TotalSegmentator code/weights](https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator).
- **Release/date evidence:** Zenodo states **10 September 2026**, v3.0.0. Dataset owner says v3 increased the collection from **1,228 to 1,939 CT images**, added 291 pediatric images, and improved/refined existing labels. The older version was public pre-cutoff, so the post-cutoff component is the new v3 data/labels, especially pediatric cases and corrected masks.
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** Clinical CT, broad whole-body anatomy; 117 anatomical structure segmentation masks.
- **Size:** 1,939 CT images; unique patient count not stated in the record. The 291 pediatric additions are explicitly counted; other newly added images account for the remaining increase, but the v3 record does not enumerate all new patient-level counts.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Clinical routine from a wide range of pathologies, scanners, sequences and institutions; exact site/vendor/country count and per-image metadata fields are not stated on Zenodo. Do not infer them from “wide range.”
- **Temporal metadata:** None specified.
- **Access/license:** Open 37.4-GB Zenodo archive; the record describes the dataset as open but the license field was not visible in the retrieved record. **Verify license on download/metadata before reuse**; code license may differ from data.
- **Test-set situation:** The full segmentation dataset is released; no hidden test split is described. Use a patient-level held-out split and avoid leakage into the agent pool.
- **Overlap risk:** It is explicitly a version extension of the old TotalSegmentator cohort; old 1,228 images overlap completely with v2. New v3 images/masks include pediatric cases, but exact overlap/addition patient IDs should be diffed. The 291 pediatric images are newly added, though whether any were present elsewhere in public datasets is unverified.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S2/S7/S8/S18/S24/S25/S30/S56.** Start from v2/nnU-Net/MedSAM2 organ weights trained on adult/older CT; target the v3 pediatric scans or improved labels with k=0/5/10/25 labeled patients. F uses strong augmentation/LoRA/standard fine-tuning; G edits anatomy-specific feature channels/decoder circuits based on probes, with source-age retention guardrails. Metrics: per-structure Dice/HD95, worst-age-group performance, old-class churn. Patchwise 3D training/inference is feasible on a 24-GB GPU; full-volume fine-tuning may not be.

### 6. Longitudinal abdominal CT with liver/metastasis masks
- **Name/links:** [Zenodo record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23349228)](https://zenodo.org/records/23349228).
- **Release/date evidence:** Zenodo says **13 October 2026**, v1. Data files (not simply the paper) are present in the record. This is post-cutoff. The record identifier in the accessible file link resolves as 23349229, while the landing page displays DOI record 23349228; use the canonical landing-page DOI and verify before downloading/citing.
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** Contrast-enhanced abdominal CT; expert-validated liver and metastasis segmentation, image registration, temporal tumor tracking and response assessment.
- **Size:** 118 CT examinations from 54 patients, with T0/pre-treatment, T1 and T2 follow-up where available. Each exam has CT volume and liver/metastasis masks; exact slice/image count not separately given.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Retrospective single-source cohort; record names an approving medical-sciences university but does not clearly identify the site. Scanner vendor/model count not provided. Metadata CSV supplies acquisition/dose parameters, sex, image quality, patient ID and timepoint.
- **Temporal metadata:** Days from baseline; T0=0, T1/T2 dates are withheld to reduce re-identification, but intervals are retained.
- **Access/license:** Public Zenodo record but files are **restricted**; log-in/access eligibility must be checked. **CC BY 4.0** license stated for data.
- **Test-set situation:** Liver and metastasis masks are provided for exams; no challenge hidden test split documented. Make patient-level held-out pairs; don’t split scans from the same patient across train/test.
- **Overlap risk:** No overlap with another named public cohort established; hospital and source identity are insufficiently explicit to rule it out. Possible overlap with local oncology/radiotherapy studies is unverified.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S5/S8/S9/S18/S24/S25/S40/S55/S56.** Start from ULS/TotalSegmentator/Merlin or nnU-Net lesions trained on single-timepoint abdominal CT; target longitudinal liver metastases with 0/1/3/5 patient trajectories. F adapts by patch fine-tuning/registration; G inserts a probe-derived temporal-difference circuit or localized tumor feature edits. Metrics: lesion Dice and lesion-wise recall, temporal correspondence/change consistency, and liver Dice. One 24-GB GPU is feasible with cropped patches; cohort is small and access is uncertain.

### 7. Spinal-Multiple-Myeloma-SEG trabecular segmentation extension
- **Name/links:** [Zenodo extension (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21628233)](https://zenodo.org/records/21628233); [original TCIA collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/spinal-multiple-myeloma-seg/); [extension paper](https://doi.org/10.59275/j.melba.2026-d741).
- **Release/date evidence:** Zenodo explicitly states **27 July 2026**, v1, and describes this as an extension to the existing TCIA collection. Newly released post-cutoff data are the corrected expert trabecular masks, not the scans.
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** Dual-energy, low-dose CT of thoracic/lumbar spine in multiple myeloma; voxel masks of trabecular bone in lumbar vertebral bodies, per L1–L5 and L6 where present. Segmentation/quantitative bone analysis.
- **Size:** 71 of the original 72 CT examinations (72 scans from 67 patients); one scan lacked the lumbar region. The extension archive is 6.1 MB of DICOM-SEG/NIfTI masks.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** TCIA collection; precise number of centers/scanner vendors and per-image metadata were not verified from the extension record. Original CT is separately hosted by TCIA and inherits TCIA terms; this mask release cannot substitute for downloading CT.
- **Temporal metadata:** The original collection includes follow-up/clinical metadata, but temporal pairing details for the extension were not verified. Do not assume repeated scans form independent patients.
- **Access/license:** Extension masks downloadable from Zenodo; the MELBA paper and Zenodo record should be used to confirm the mask license (CC BY 4.0 is indicated in the study/challenge materials); CT requires TCIA access/terms. Treat licensing of masks and source scans separately.
- **Test-set situation:** Masks are released; no hidden test set stated. Patient-level cross-validation is appropriate for the 67-patient cohort.
- **Overlap risk:** 100% image overlap with the previously available Spinal-Multiple-Myeloma-SEG CT cohort; only trabecular labels are new. The original collection’s vertebra and tumor masks also overlap for the same scans.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S2/S8/S24/S25/S56**, plus **S49** dual-energy protocol shifts. Start from CT bone/vertebral segmentation weights; target trabecular compartment/energy-channel labels with k=1/3/5 cases. F trains a small segmentation head; G uses vertebral- and trabecular-feature probes to edit the decoder/feature routing while preserving myeloma-lesion performance. Dice/HD95 and bone-density/lesion stratum performance; patchwise 3D feasible on 24 GB, but small n makes this a mechanistic rather than high-power benchmark.

### 8. LUCQUE (Québec LDCT model-to-data resource) — public timing/access needs confirmation
- **Name/links:** [arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12817); [PARADIM project/data listing](https://paradim.science/actifs-sous-gestion/).
- **Release/date evidence:** The first verified public description is arXiv v1 posted **9 November 2026**, and the PARADIM page lists LUCQUE. The abstract says the dataset is currently accessible via PARADIM model-to-data. It does **not** establish the exact first date on which the dataset itself became available on PARADIM; therefore this is a **provisional post-cutoff entry**, not fully verified under the data-first-release rule. Confirm with PARADIM before treating it as eligible.
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** Low-dose chest CT lung-cancer-screening baseline studies; expert voxel-level nodule and cyst masks and Lung-RADS-based lesion characteristics; detection/segmentation and screening evaluation.
- **Size:** 1,678 baseline studies from 1,678 participants; 1,776 nodules and 60 cysts. PARADIM’s general listing says the managed LUCQUE project has 3,000 participants, so the 1,678 paper cohort may be a subset/current release; reconcile before planning power.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Québec, Canada; exact hospital/site and scanner-vendor counts are not given in the abstract. Paper reports demographics, imaging parameters, radiological findings and lesion characteristics. PARADIM is a privacy-preserving infrastructure.
- **Temporal metadata:** The paper reports baseline studies only; longitudinal follow-up for the cohort was not established in the retrieved abstract.
- **Access/license:** Not a direct image download: access is by **model-to-data** execution on PARADIM so data stay on-premise. Registration/DUA or project authorization is likely; license not specified in the retrieved abstract. Confirm terms and approved code/compute environment.
- **Test-set situation:** Not described as a conventional hidden challenge split. Evaluation is constrained by the platform/model-to-data workflow; no image export. Access to labels and evaluation protocol needs confirmation.
- **Overlap risk:** No known exact overlap with LIDC/NLST or other public datasets is reported; it is a local Québec screening cohort. Verify patient-level separation from any provincial screening data used elsewhere.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S1/S5/S8/S10/S34/S41/S49.** Use a locally pretrained/fine-tuned lung-nodule detector and submit the fixed model or adaptation program to a model-to-data evaluator. Compare F k-shot/augmentation/LoRA with G’s probe-guided nodule-feature/kernal edits under the same allowed labels; score FROC sensitivity at 1/4 FP per scan and calibration. The 24-GB feasibility depends on PARADIM’s permitted runtime/hardware, not just model size.

### 9. Derived patch-level lung nodule classification data (low-priority but post-cutoff artifact)
- **Name/links:** [Zenodo: Processed patch-level CT dataset for lung nodule classification](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22926100).
- **Release/date evidence:** Zenodo search record gives **23 September 2026** for the processed dataset. It is a new derived artifact; source CT images and annotations are pre-cutoff datasets.
- **Modality/anatomy; tasks/labels:** 2.5D 64×64×3 CT patches labeled non-nodule/benign/malignant; source collections LUNA16, LIDC-IDRI and LNDb. Classification, not full-volume segmentation/detection.
- **Size:** The release summary does not state reliable patient/image counts in the retrieved record; it includes extracted CNN features/checkpoints/results. **Verify counts and patient grouping from the record before use.**
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Inherited heterogeneous sites/vendors from three parent datasets; exact per-patch vendor/site/demographic metadata and country counts not verified. Patient-disjoint split is claimed in the record summary.
- **Temporal metadata:** Inherited source datasets, but no unified longitudinal scheme verified.
- **Access/license:** Zenodo open record; exact license should be checked in the record metadata before reuse.
- **Test-set situation:** Reported patient-disjoint benchmark partitions, but test labels appear packaged with the data; treat as released rather than hidden.
- **Overlap risk:** **Exact source overlap** with LUNA16/LIDC-IDRI/LNDb; severe contamination risk if agents know these benchmarks. It adds processed patches/features rather than new patients, and preprocessing may leak label selection cues.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S5/S22/S34.** Cheap feature/weight-edit tests only. F fine-tunes 2.5D classifier; G edits nodule-feature directions, with held-out patients. AUROC/FROC proxies; 24-GB GPU easy. Poor priority because patch extraction loses volume context and the parent data are well known.

## Borderline: data first released January–June 2026 (not qualifying for the primary pool)

1. **AMPLIFAI — multiphase LI-RADS CT.** [Official challenge data page](https://um-ihc-ca2i.github.io/amplifai-challenge/data.html), [timeline](https://um-ihc-ca2i.github.io/amplifai-challenge/timeline.html), [HF files](https://huggingface.co/datasets/UM-IHC-CA2i/AMPLIFAI), [dataset paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14778). The official timeline says training batches released **5, 19 and 26 June 2026**, so its data itself predates cutoff, even though the paper/HF listing appeared later. Official current challenge description: 764 multiphase CT cases, split into training/validation/test; arXiv preprint reports 590 studies, while an earlier Zenodo paper abstract reports 668 cases—version/count discrepancies require snapshot citation. Liver lesions, LI-RADS case-level classification, voxel lesion masks and feature masks; phases arterial/portal venous/delayed/noncontrast where available. Four public source datasets were harmonized, so overlap with the source datasets is direct. Gated Hugging Face access requires registration/terms; current challenge page says noncommercial research and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Public validation labels (59 cases); challenge test labels are hidden. Use only for a borderline/pre-cutoff analysis, not the strict primary benchmark.

2. **ReXGroundingCT.** [NEJM AI paper](https://doi.org/10.1056/aidbp2501220), [HF data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkarlab/ReXGroundingCT), [MICCAI challenge archive](https://rexrank.ai/ReXGroundingCT/challenge.html). Dataset paper was public **18 June 2026**, before cutoff; actual file first-release date was not independently established, so treat as borderline. It contains 3,142 noncontrast chest CT scans and 8,028 finding masks across 14 categories, 2,992 train, 50 public validation, and 100 private/hidden cases. It grounds text to 3D masks; overlaps CT-RATE images/reports (source data are older), creating complete image-overlap risk. License/access details should be checked on the HF card. Excellent segmentation/VLM data, but not eligible unless a repository log proves data files first went live after June.

3. **Lung tumor CT segmentation dataset (ScienceDB).** [ScienceDB record DOI](https://doi.org/10.57760/sciencedb.29318), [Scientific Data descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07963-0). Search result for the dataset portal gives **7 March 2026**; descriptor published 28 July is later and does not change data availability. Two image cohorts (early-stage 730, advanced-stage 449; 1,179 cases total per descriptor) with radiologist/radiation-oncologist tumor masks. Single clinical collaboration at Anhui Chest Hospital is described, but scanner/site metadata and license were not confirmed from retrieved full text. Data use in a new challenge requires checking original publication/ScienceDB terms; no sealed test split established. It is an informative near-threshold lung-tumor segmentation set, but pre-cutoff.

4. **TotalSegmentator liver_lesions training data.** [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20272572](https://zenodo.org/records/20272572). Zenodo search index dates it **18 May 2026**. 842 CT cases with liver-lesion masks, nnU-Net format; liver/lesion segmentation and oncology. Site/vendor/time metadata and license were not verified. Labels appear released with the training set, no hidden test. Likely overlaps publicly available CT cohorts; audit case IDs. Strong candidate for S24/S25/S56 and R1/R2 low-label lesion work, but pre-cutoff.

5. **PatchChestCT.** [Scientific Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07793-0), [Zenodo v3](https://zenodo.org/records/19707049). Zenodo states release **23 April 2026**. New patch-level masks on 2,201 noncontrast chest CT scans for nine abnormalities; train/validation/test split details need the paper. Includes spatial labels rather than image-level labels; scanner/site metadata and exact license/access should be checked. Pre-cutoff; any overlap with source chest CTs must be resolved.

6. **LIDC-annot-NLST501.** [TCIA analysis result](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/analysis-result/lidc-annot-nlst501/), [TCIA page](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/analysis-result/lidc-annot-nlst501/). Exa results show a 6 February 2026 dataset record but TCIA lists an update on 5 August 2026; annotation campaign itself was 2022–2023. First public file release is therefore **ambiguous**; earliest evidence is pre-cutoff, so do not count it as post-cutoff without deposit history. 501 NLST patients (152 cancer, 349 non-cancer), multi-reader annotations: 6,505 segmented nodules, 10,042 target lesions and 12,968 non-target lesions; low-dose chest CT, four vendors (GE/Philips/Siemens/Toshiba), source images from NLST. Up to three longitudinal annual NLST timepoints exist, although annotations use selected timepoint. The NLST image collection is older, but the annotations are new; strong direct overlap risk with NLST and Ardila 2019 (paper says 410 shared patients). TCIA access/terms apply; annotations appear released.

7. **LUNOVO26 debug set.** [Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19449567). Data published **7 April 2026**; five debugging cases are derived directly from LIDC-IDRI (older images), not a new independent cohort. It is only an inference/debugging package for nodule volumetry; no meaningful independent test labels. CC BY 3.0 applies to source LIDC. Borderline and low utility for the strict experiment.

8. **ReX/other 2026 datasets whose actual release timing remains unresolved:** [MAGIC-CT](https://zenodo.org/records/18389015) is an oncology abdominal CT resource (562 patients, lesion/organ masks, multi-language reports) but the visible Zenodo page only says “Published 2026” and requires access/DUA/training; no day was verified. [CTSpinoPelvic1K v7](https://zenodo.org/records/22242745) is dated 2 September 2026 and contains new annotation/mapping labels for 802 old TCIA CT records, but which annotation fields first appeared in a post-cutoff version (v7 vs earlier releases) is unclear. [MOOSE/TotalSegmentator anatomy masks for CPTAC/CMB CT](https://zenodo.org/records/23825859) was deposited 2 November 2026, but is AI-derived annotation/radiomics on pre-existing TCIA cohorts; counts, license, hidden-test status and patient-level overlap need inspection. Keep these as conditional leads, not clean independent datasets. MAGIC-CT reports 562 patients with masks, about 1,250 lesions and 4,937 organ descriptions in Russian/Kazakh/English for 492 patients; file access carries a DUA. The underlying scans are contrast CT from existing institutions, but site/vendor/timepoint details are not verified in the retrieved record.

9. **Longitudinal-CT melanoma dataset is NOT eligible.** Although its paper appeared in 2026 and a later Zenodo/FDAT record is visible, the FDAT DOI lists an earlier **16 September 2025** release (and a 2025 v2 record); the data were publicly available before cutoff. It is 300 patients/600 baseline-follow-up whole-body CT studies, 7,182 lesions, one Tübingen site, Siemens scanners, CC BY. Do not treat the 2026 paper/record refresh as a new dataset without a newly released data partition. [Scientific Data article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07466-y); [FDAT record](https://fdat.uni-tuebingen.de/records/75kj1-64747).

## Concrete experimental setups enabled

Every comparison must start from the **same byte-identical checkpoint**, share labels/unlabeled data/compute/token budgets, include a random-edit and mechanical-WSP baseline, and report edit-reversion contribution and source retention. Seal evaluation data where feasible, deduplicate at patient level, and perform a site-from-image/prevalence confound audit.

1. **Routine CT → pelvic CBCT reconstruction/hardware shift (COBRA2026; S5/S8/S12/S49).** Start: CT denoising/reconstruction model or CT-organ segmenter and a fixed preprocessing pipeline. Source: diagnostic/planning CT and simulated projections; target: measured CBCT from held-out center/vendor. Target regimes: unlabeled target CBCT and 5/20 reviewed cases; **F** gets physics-aware standard reconstruction, LoRA/adapter, augmentation; **G** additionally gets probe/SAE-localized projection or stem edits and sparse vendor-specific feature rescaling. Metric: projection/volume error, SSIM, HU error, paired reference consistency; on a separately annotated subset, organ Dice. Why practitioners care: new CBCT systems arrive without large label sets. **24 GB:** yes for patch/2D/limited projection training; not full 950-GB end-to-end data.

2. **Multicancer screening external shift and temporal drift (CancerVerse; S1/S7/S9/S55).** Start: nnU-Net/CT-FM/Merlin-like model trained on older public cancer data or a locked CancerVerse source-hospital split. Source: earlier time periods/site(s); target: held-out site, country, or prediagnosis interval, with 0/5/10/25/50 target patients. **F** uses strong 3D augmentation, LoRA, temporal baselines and calibrates threshold; **G** uses unlabeled-target probes to edit site directions, or inserts a temporal-difference circuit. Metric: lesion sensitivity at 0.25/0.5 FP per scan (primary), Dice, worst-site sensitivity, calibration and churn. Why: screening utility depends on high sensitivity with very low FP. **24 GB:** yes using patch-based 3D/ROI training; full 3-TB dataset is not needed.

3. **Add multi-cancer / rare tumor labels without forgetting (CancerVerse; S22/S24/S25/S56).** Start: a locked abdominal CT segmenter trained on liver/kidney/pancreas labels; targets include new organs with sparse masks (e.g. gallbladder, duodenum, uterus) and 0/1/5/20 target patients per cancer. **F** adds a head/decoder or LoRA and uses rehearsal if permitted; **G** adds interpretability-derived feature-to-logit circuits and sparse decoder edits, preserving old outputs. Metric: new-type lesion sensitivity/Dice and old-class Dice/churn. Why: practitioners need to add capabilities while retaining validated organ coverage. **24 GB:** yes patchwise, with class-balanced sampling; 13-class full volumes require careful memory management.

4. **Coronary-tree anatomy capability and topology (ImageCAS-X; S24/S25/S32/S56).** Start: CT-FM/MedSAM2/TotalSegmentator or a frozen natural-image DINOv3 encoder plus 3D decoder. Source: generic CT/organ or older ImageCAS labels; target: 560 training CCTA cases with new segment labels; k=1/5/20/100 patients. **F** trains a 3D vessel decoder and topology loss; **G** adds probe-guided coronary feature circuits and an edited tree/segment head. Metric: Dice, clDice, centerline distance, per-branch recall and topology error; retain other structure performance. Why: coronary quantitative measurements and CT-FFR depend on continuity. **24 GB:** feasible with vessel crops/patches; full 512×512 volumes are not.

5. **Pediatric anatomy/domain generalization (TotalSegmentator v3; S2/S7/S8/S30).** Start: same v2/older adult organ segmenter; target: v3 pediatric CT and pediatric-only labels, with 0/5/10/25 target scans and unlabeled target images. **F** uses scale/intensity augmentation, LoRA, BN/statistics adaptation; **G** edits age/size-responsive shape and anatomy directions localized by probes. Metric: per-organ Dice/HD95, worst-age-bin performance, source adult retention and prediction churn. Why: a deployed adult CT model may fail on pediatric body-size/anatomy. **24 GB:** yes on 3D patches; test set should be newly patient split, not v3 training data exposed to agents.

6. **Longitudinal liver metastasis maintenance (abdominal CT 54-patient set; S9/S24/S55).** Start: ULS/nnU-Net/Merlin lesion model trained on single-timepoint CT. Source: baseline T0; target: T1/T2 response scans, with 0/1/3/5 patient trajectories. **F** registration plus temporal fine-tuning; **G** adds an interpretable lesion-change/difference circuit and local edits to maintain lesion identity under shrinkage, disappearance, merging/splitting. Metric: lesion Dice/recall, lesion correspondence F1, change class accuracy and retention on liver masks. Why: treatment response and measurement changes are everyday oncology tasks. **24 GB:** yes with lesion crops; 54 patients is small and cannot power 0.01 AUROC claims.

7. **Low-dose nodule external evaluation under data governance (LUCQUE, conditional; S1/S8/S10/S34).** Start: locked low-dose CT nodule model trained on public NLST/LIDC; source → Québec screening cohort; target regimes R0 (no image access), platform-approved unlabeled evaluation, or authorized k-shot labels if PARADIM allows. **F** uploads a standard adapter/fine-tune pipeline; **G** includes interpretability-based nodule/kernel edits. Metric: FROC sensitivity at fixed FP/scan and nodule volume error; use site-separated cohort if available. Why: represents real deployment evaluation without moving protected images. **24 GB:** unknown until PARADIM runtime rules are confirmed. Not eligible for the final eligible pool until the actual first data-availability date is documented.

## Contradictions and cautions

- CancerVerse’s HF page reflects a later v3.5 snapshot (24,422 scans/14,599 patients), whereas the MICCAI paper reports the earlier v3 counts (24,168/13,863). Its page says the first public release was July 2026, but earlier versions were internal; cite the snapshot and do not mix counts.
- AMPLIFAI is a clear date-boundary trap: papers appeared August, but official timeline says training data batches were released June 5–26. The paper/HF date must not be substituted for first data release. Counts vary (590/668/764) by artifact/version.
- ImageCAS-X is post-cutoff as an annotation release but its images are an 800-case subset of the older ImageCAS benchmark. It is not independent imaging data.
- The longitudinal melanoma CT paper is 2026, but the data DOI is 2025; reject for strict cutoff.
- TotalSegmentator v3 is a valid new *version/extension* per the brief, not a newly collected independent dataset; patient IDs and newly added cases must be isolated before splitting.
- CBCT-IQ is phantom-only; it is a controlled quality benchmark, not evidence of patient performance.
- COBRA paired planning CTs are deformably registered/simulated references rather than perfectly matched same-time ground truth; do not interpret downstream metrics as absolute diagnostic accuracy.
- WSP edit success must be attributed by reverting edits after training; include random edits, mechanical baselines, and same label budgets. Do not frame this shard as shortcut removal.

## Missing evidence / residual risks

- Exact first downloadable dates are unavailable for LUCQUE and MAGIC-CT; LUCQUE’s first public description is November 9, 2026 but PARADIM may have hosted the data earlier. MAGIC-CT only exposes year-level release in the fetched Zenodo page.
- License metadata and source-image agreements need independent verification for TotalSegmentator v3, CT-SpinoPelvic1K, processed patch-level CT, and LUCQUE. For multi-source derived resources, check each inherited image license as well as mask/license terms.
- Scanner/vendor/site counts and per-image demographics are absent or incomplete for several datasets (TotalSegmentator v3, abdominal liver set, ImageCAS-X labels, spinal extension, LUCQUE); do not infer them from the article affiliations.
- Exa source-check validation was attempted for COBRA2026, but the Exa backend rate-limited subsequent requests (HTTP 429). The record and paper were fetched directly and manually inspected; the claim is supported by those primary pages, but the secondary source-check routine could not retrieve passages.
- Confirm authorized access/DUA for restricted abdominal CT and secure-compute use for LUCQUE before committing an experiment. Confirm all snapshots/checkpoints are frozen before agents see them.

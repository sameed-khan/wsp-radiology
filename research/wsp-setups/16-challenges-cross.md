# Cross-cutting challenges, shift benchmarks, and new radiology data (as of 2026-11-12)

## Executive summary

Three post-cutoff assets stand out as practically relevant and sufficiently evidenced: **COBRA2026** (pelvic radiotherapy CBCT; data release 2026-07-13), **TopBrain's August 2026 36-class vessel-label extension** (the image cases are older; only the new annotation extension is post-cutoff), and the **RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection Challenge** (officially launched 2026-08-05, with 5,000+ knee MRI exams and multilingual reports). A fourth post-cutoff Zenodo record, **CLEAR-EC**, is ophthalmic and has restricted access/no publicly downloadable files, so it is not presently a usable public dataset. I found no verifiable post-cutoff pediatric dataset from Africa/South Asia/Latin America; LISA and TREAT-MMTB are promising but their *image-data* release dates and general-public access remain unverified.

Challenge listings are much more complete than their per-dataset provenance. Dates below are for the data itself when verified; challenge-paper or challenge-launch dates are explicitly not substituted for first image-data release. For the larger challenge inventory, missing data metadata is not evidence that no data exist.

## Method and scope notes

- Cutoff applied exactly as requested: image/label data must have first become publicly available on or after 2026-07-01. Paper publication, challenge announcement, dataset-description deposit, and an updated leaderboard alone do not prove image-data release.
- I enumerated the official MICCAI 2026 challenge program, the current Grand Challenge 2026 index, ISBI challenge pages, RSNA, candidate Kaggle competitions, and the NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations & Datasets (formerly Datasets & Benchmarks) track. Discovery used source-check plus direct page fetches; **the required `exa_search` tool was not exposed in this runtime**. I did not use the brief-prohibited `web_search`. Kaggle pages are JavaScript-rendered and their direct content could not be read; Kaggle candidates below are consequently leads, not verified datasets.
- Each asset's 11 requested fields are included below. “Unknown/not reported” means I did not verify it on a source, not that the attribute is absent.

## Post-cutoff dataset assets / extensions

### 1. COBRA2026 pelvic CBCT projection dataset — verified eligible; strongest shift-study candidate

1. **Name and links:** [Zenodo dataset record](https://zenodo.org/records/21322350) / [dataset paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20037) / [COBRA2026 Grand Challenge](https://cobra2026.grand-challenge.org/).
2. **Data release:** **2026-07-13.** Zenodo API record states created/publication date 2026-07-13, and its dataset description gives the training-set release date as 13.07.2026. This is data, not just the challenge or paper date; training download links and split sizes are listed in the dataset record. Source: [Zenodo API metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/21322350).
3. **Modality/anatomy:** Raw radiotherapy cone-beam CT (CBCT) X-ray projections and reconstructed pelvic volumes, paired with planning CT; pelvis, radiotherapy.
4. **Task/labels:** Image reconstruction and image correction (not one of the requested classification/segmentation/detection/report labels). Paired planning CT and simulated projection data provide references; registration and temporal anatomy changes mean planning CT is not a perfect voxelwise truth for every real daily CBCT. No segmentation/classification labels reported.
5. **Size:** 867 cases/patients total: 692 train, 52 validation, 123 test; approximately 950 GB. Each case contains projection stacks and volumetric images. The training release is ~750 GB.
6. **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Six European centers in four countries (Germany, Netherlands, France, Austria); two imaging-system vendors, Elekta and Varian. Per-case ID encodes center. XML/YAML metadata include acquisition geometry, acquisition/reconstruction/imaging parameters and vendor-specific calibration/correction information. Scanner model counts and patient demographics are not reported in the fetched dataset description.
7. **Temporal metadata:** The clinical problem involves daily CBCT during a radiotherapy course and a planning CT at an earlier time, but whether anonymized per-image dates/fraction numbers are available was not verified. Do not assume longitudinal patient linkage beyond described paired planning CT and daily CBCT.
8. **Access/license:** Training data are open through an institutional repository; dataset page says no account/registration is required. **CC BY-NC 4.0** (attribution/non-commercial); verify terms before downstream commercial use.
9. **Test set:** Challenge evaluation uses a hidden test set. Dataset record lists validation and test but states their download release is 2032-03-01; currently the released training split is the actionable asset.
10. **Overlap risk:** No overlap with a named pre-cutoff public dataset is reported. The new 2026 collection is distinct, but patient-level cross-dataset overlap cannot be ruled out from the public description. Do not train on challenge test data.
11. **WSP setups:** **S7/S8/S40/S49**. Train a fixed reconstruction model on selected source centers/vendor, then test held-out centers or Elekta↔Varian shift; R1 unlabeled target projections or small R2 paired labeled target volumes. Use a 2D/3D reconstruction network/UNet as identical starting checkpoint; F uses fine-tuning/LoRA, data augmentation and standard reconstruction baselines; G must additionally make logged probe/SAE-guided edits to identifiable vendor/site-sensitive encoder or normalization channels. Evaluate MAE in HU, PSNR/SSIM, target/source retention and prediction/image churn. Practical caveat: full dataset does not fit a single-GPU workflow; a cropped/patch-based subset does.

### 2. TopBrain August 2026 label extension (CTA/MRA cerebral vessel anatomy) — eligible *extension*, not new images

1. **Name and links:** [TopBrain Challenge Data Release (Zenodo)](https://zenodo.org/records/21972006); [TopBrain v2 challenge](https://topbrain2026.grand-challenge.org/); [TopAneu 2026](https://topaneu-26.grand-challenge.org/); [original TopCoW challenge/data source](https://topcow24.grand-challenge.org/).
2. **Data release:** **2026-08-17** for the new record and its added labels. Zenodo metadata says the August release adds the 36-class TopAneu annotations, including two new infraclinoid-vessel classes. This qualifies as an extension under the brief's rule; the scans themselves predate the cutoff. Source: [Zenodo API metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/21972006).
3. **Modality/anatomy:** 3D CTA and MRA of intracranial/whole-brain vessels.
4. **Task/labels:** Voxel-level multi-class vessel segmentation; 36-class vessel labels (plus other TopBrain vessel anatomy labels in the package).
5. **Size:** **50 volumes, described as 25 CTA/MRA pairs**; the new 36-class labels are added to existing training cases. The release archive is about 1.96 GB. Exact unique-patient count is not separately stated; “25 pairs” implies paired cases, not necessarily a verified patient count.
6. **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Number of sites, scanner manufacturers/models, collection countries, and per-image site/device/demographic metadata are not specified in the dataset release description.
7. **Temporal metadata:** None reported.
8. **Access/license:** Open download; terms allow non-commercial use and require attribution, with prior permission required for commercial use (not a standard unrestricted commercial license).
9. **Test set:** TopBrain/TopAneu evaluation includes hidden annotated test data; the public record is the training release. Do not treat the challenge hidden test as downloadable labels.
10. **Overlap risk:** **High and explicit.** The image data are the TopCoW CTA/MRA cases from 2025, and most vessel classes overlap the earlier 2025 TopBrain/TopCoW labels. Only the new two infraclinoid classes / newly released TA36 labels are new after cutoff. A model trained on the August TA36 Docker has already seen this post-cutoff label extension and is not a clean pretrained starting point for a memorization-resistant study.
11. **WSP setups:** **S24/S25/S2**. Start from a pre-cutoff vessel segmenter (e.g., a 2025 TopBrain/TopCoW checkpoint, if its provenance is confirmed) or MedSAM2; source = common artery classes, target = two new infraclinoid vessel classes or modality transfer CTA→MRA, with k=1/5/10 paired cases. F uses frozen-head/new-head, LoRA or fine-tuning; G uses F plus an interpretability-derived decoder/feature edit to wire vessel-concept features into new logits while preserving old classes. Score new-class Dice/HD95, old-class Dice, classwise churn. 24 GB feasibility: likely for patch-wise 3D inference/training on this small dataset, but verify volume sizes and GPU memory. **Power warning:** 25 paired cases are too few for precise subgroup/domain-generalization estimates; treat as mechanistic pilot, not a definitive multi-site benchmark.

1. **Name and links:** [RSNA challenge dataset/program page](https://www.rsna.org/artificial-intelligence/ai-image-challenge/knee-mri-ai-challenge), [RSNA launch announcement (2026-08-05)](https://www.rsna.org/news/2026/august/ai-challenge-knee-mri), [Kaggle competition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection/overview).
2. **Data release:** The official RSNA announcement says the challenge launched **2026-08-05**, and challenge data were provided through Kaggle. This verifies that the challenge/data became available after cutoff, but the exact Kaggle file-upload timestamp was not independently exposed; use 2026-08-05 as the earliest official public access date, not as a verified per-file timestamp.
3. **Modality/anatomy:** Knee MRI exams plus associated radiology reports in 12 languages.
4. **Task/labels:** Detection/classification of knee abnormalities; image and report multimodal task. The challenge page says expert-radiologist annotations are used for performance evaluation. No segmentation masks are reported.
5. **Size:** More than 5,000 exams; patient count, number of MRI image series/slices, and label counts were not verified in official content.
6. **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** Official RSNA sources conflict: the August launch release says 16 sites; the current challenge page says 19. Both call it worldwide/across five continents. Twelve report languages are reported. Per-study site IDs, scanner vendors/models, acquisition dates, country metadata, and availability of the 16/19 site identifiers were not verified.
7. **Temporal metadata:** Not reported.
8. **Access/license:** Competition hosted on Kaggle and open to researchers, requiring Kaggle access/acceptance of competition rules. An open data license for the images/reports was not found; competition participation terms govern. Do not assume unrestricted redistribution or commercial reuse.
9. **Test set:** Challenge test labels are hidden; performance is scored in competition. Kaggle data page could not be fetched because it is JavaScript-rendered, so exact public training/validation file split and current continued access remain unverified.
10. **Overlap risk:** No overlap with named pre-cutoff knee MRI collections was established from official sources. Treat overlap as unknown; audit for reused cases and pretrained-model exposure before study.
11. **WSP setups:** **S1/S7/S48** (and S45 if paired with an encoder upgrade). Potentially train on source sites/languages and hold out other sites/languages, with k-shot target adaptation; use a fixed medical MRI encoder or DINOv3 ViT-B as a generalist starting point. F uses LP-FT/LoRA, multilingual augmentation and report-assisted training. G does the same plus logged interpretability-derived edits that align language/concept or site-sensitive directions while maintaining class locality. Primary metric: macro-AUPRC/AUROC and worst-language/site score; guardrails: source-site performance and churn. Feasibility: a 2D slice encoder + study aggregation is feasible on one 24 GB GPU; full 3D MRI end-to-end fine-tuning may not be. **Condition:** clean cross-site analysis requires public per-study site labels; if Kaggle files do not expose site metadata, use only demonstrable report-language strata or do not claim a site-held-out experiment.

## Concrete WSP experimental setups enabled by this shard

Use the same byte-identical starting model, equal GPU/agent budgets, sealed patient-level test splits, and a model-diff log in both arms. Arm G is not “just fine-tuning”: it must make explicit, reproducible interpretability-derived weight edits. Revert logged edits individually and together; include matched random-direction edits and a mechanical baseline, per the common design brief.

### Setup 1 — Multicenter CBCT reconstruction adaptation (COBRA2026)

- **Backbone/model:** source-trained 2D/3D reconstruction U-Net (or an unrolled reconstruction network) with a fixed projection/geometry pipeline; do not use the challenge hidden test. Train the initial model on a subset of the released source centers/vendors.
- **Source → target:** source sites / Elekta → held-out center or Varian (and reverse in a replicate). Use train-only sites for source, reserve patient-level samples in a target site for R1 unlabeled or R2 few-shot paired reference supervision.
- **F:** full standard pipeline—fine-tune/LoRA, CBCT augmentation, normalization recalibration and reconstruction baselines.
- **G:** F plus a logged probe/SAE-guided edit to identified vendor/site encoding channels, stem/residual filters or norm statistics; edit contribution must survive revert and random-edit controls.
- **Metric:** HU MAE, PSNR/SSIM; target-site gain, source-site retention, HU bias and prediction/image churn. Because planning CT is not a perfect same-time reference, report simulated-pair results separately from real CBCT results.
- **Why it matters:** image quality and HU stability across installed linacs/venders determines whether a CBCT can support adaptive radiotherapy rather than just positioning.
- **24 GB:** **Feasible on selected volumes/patches**, not full 950-GB data or full-volume end-to-end training. The full public train corpus itself is ~750 GB.

### Setup 2 — Vascular segmentation increment without forgetting (TopBrain TA36 extension)

- **Backbone/model:** a clean pre-cutoff TopBrain/TopCoW 2025 3D CTA/MRA vessel segmenter (provenance audited) or MedSAM2; avoid the August 2026 TA36 docker because it has trained on post-cutoff labels.
- **Source → target:** common existing vessel classes → two newly labeled infraclinoid classes; optionally CTA → paired MRA. k=1/5/10 annotated pairs, with other public training cases withheld from target training.
- **F:** new output classes plus standard head/decoder fine-tuning, LoRA, class-balanced loss and augmentation; report old-class performance.
- **G:** F plus a feature-/attribution-guided direct edit that routes identifiable vessel-concept channels into new class logits, preserving old logits/weights as much as possible. Log tensor, magnitude, probe/attribution evidence, and ablate each edit.
- **Metric:** Dice/HD95 for each added class; old-class Dice retention; class-specific prediction churn and changed-parameter fraction.
- **Why it matters:** real clinical model maintenance often adds anatomically meaningful structures while preserving validated segmentation behavior.
- **24 GB:** **Likely feasible patch-wise**, but confirm actual image dimensions and memory. Only 25 paired cases means exploratory mechanistic results, not adequately powered generalization claims.

### Setup 3 — Multilingual/multicenter knee MRI abnormality recognition (RSNA 2026)

- **Backbone/model:** DINOv3 ViT-B or a pre-cutoff medical-image encoder for MRI slices plus a report-text encoder; image/report fusion and patient-level study pooling are fixed across arms. Verify model/data policy before using any external corpus.
- **Source → target:** source-language/site portion of RSNA train data → a held-out language/site in the same public training split; k=5/10/25 target studies plus an unlabeled target pool. Use the competition hidden test only for final official evaluation, not adaptation.
- **F:** standard LoRA/LP-FT, multilingual text/image augmentation and report-assisted supervision.
- **G:** F plus interpretability-derived edits to language- or site-sensitive feature directions in the shared projection/fusion layers, targeting cross-language alignment while preserving unrelated findings. If site/language metadata are absent, do not label a split as “site-held-out”; first verify metadata, or use only clearly inferable report-language groups.
- **Metric:** macro-AUPRC/AUROC, per-language/per-site worst-group metric, calibration, and source-language retention/churn.
- **Why it matters:** MRI reports and acquisition vary internationally; models need reliable abnormality triage outside one hospital/language.
- **24 GB:** **Feasible** for a 2D slice encoder and modest text model with low batch size/LoRA; full 3D MRI VLM fine-tuning is not assumed feasible.

### Setup 4 — Gated ophthalmic transfer (CLEAR-EC; only after access approval)

- **Backbone/model:** small image encoder or OCT/ophthalmic feature model; data access and site labels must be confirmed first.
- **Source → target:** eye-bank source to held-out eye bank, with k-shot labels for cell density/CV/hexagonality.
- **F:** regression fine-tuning, calibration, image-quality augmentation. **G:** F plus image-feature probe-derived sparse edits for cell morphology/quality directions.
- **Metric:** per-output MAE/absolute-percent error and worst-bank error.
- **Why it matters:** transferable measurements may reduce operator dependence in graft assessment.
- **24 GB:** probably feasible once images/count/access are known; currently **not runnable** because Zenodo marks record restricted and provides no files.

## Contradictions and material uncertainties

1. **RSNA site count:** official August launch release reports 16 sites; current RSNA challenge page says 19 sites. Use “16–19; official pages conflict” until RSNA clarifies. Countries are described as five continents but not enumerated in the inspected official material.
2. **CLEAR-EC access:** challenge page says training data are publicly available at Zenodo, but Zenodo API identifies the record as `restricted` and lists zero files. Treat access as restricted until files/access instructions are actually available.
3. **ISLES/ATLAS new-data timing:** challenge description is dated April 29, 2026; current site says the 1,453-case set is released, but no image-data first-release timestamp was found. The description date cannot be used to date the image release.
4. **COBRA temporal pairing:** page discusses planning CT versus daily CBCT, but exact per-image dates/fraction metadata and patient-level repeat-scan structure were not confirmed.
5. **MICCAI/Grand Challenge dataset release dates:** listing an event as “2026,” or having challenge dates in 2026, does not show when its images/labels first became publicly available. The 44-event inventory is not a claim that all 44 data releases are cutoff-eligible.

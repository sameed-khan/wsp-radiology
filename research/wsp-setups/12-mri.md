# MRI datasets released after 2026-07-01: research brief

**Research date:** 2026-11-12. **Cutoff:** first public availability of the data itself on/after 2026-07-01; new labels/versions/extensions count only for their genuinely new portion. Paper date alone is not treated as a data-release date.

## Summary

Five high-confidence, directly useful MRI resources or extensions were verifiably public after the cutoff: the July TopAneu second batch, August TopBrain vessel-label release, September TotalSegmentator-MRI v3 extension, October MAMA-MIAAI breast-mask/bias-correction extension, and November OpenBrain generated-parcellation release. Several reuse scans already public before the cutoff, so the eligible novelty is specifically the newly released images, annotations, or derived data—not all images in the bundle. CFB-GBM v2.0 and OpticNerveSeg are promising but kept conditional because the exact first availability of their data payload was not verified; a separate Jan–Jun 2026 borderline list follows.

## Dataset findings

### 1. TopAneu 2026 — Batch 2 (include only the post-cutoff tranche)

1. **Name/links:** [TopAneu data page](https://topaneu-26.grand-challenge.org/data/) and its [SwitchDrive training-data link](https://drive.switch.ch/index.php/s/O36U43RkChkNcHd).
2. **Release date/evidence:** Batch 2 released **2026-07-31**, explicitly dated on the challenge data page. Batch 1 was released 2026-06-15 and is borderline, not post-cutoff.
3. **Modality/anatomy:** 3D TOF-MRA and CTA; intracranial arteries, aneurysms.
4. **Task/labels:** Aneurysm detection/localization and voxel masks: multiclass aneurysm-location masks, aneurysm-type masks (saccular/dissecting/fusiform), JSON locations; vessel masks are organizer-model predictions. Segmentation and detection/localization, with categorical subtype labels.
5. **Size:** Full training bundle = **415 scans / 408 unique patients**. Batch 2 adds **318 scans**: 199 MRA (center 1), 47 CTA (center 2), 4 CTA (center 4), and 68 MRA (public center 5). Thus, **250 newly contributed clinical-center scans** are clearly post-cutoff acquisitions; the 68 center-5 scans derive from pre-existing public INSTED/OpenNeuro datasets and should be excluded from a strict image-novelty test or reported separately. Exact new-batch unique-patient count is not stated; seven center-4 patients have longitudinal scans.
6. **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** Four numbered sources in the full release: CHUV/Lausanne and HUG/Geneva (Switzerland), Mie-Chuo (Japan), plus public center 5. UMC Utrecht is center 3 and is reserved for test. Center ID and modality are encoded per scan. Scanner vendor/field strength and demographic coverage are not specified on the data page. Center-4 longitudinal-order metadata is explicitly not chronological.
7. **Temporal metadata:** Seven center-4 patients have longitudinal scans; file sequence suffixes do not indicate chronology. No broader follow-up metadata specified.
8. **Access/license:** SwitchDrive download; terms permit non-commercial research with source attribution; commercial use requires prior permission. No DUA described.
9. **Test set:** Center-3 UMC Utrecht scans excluded from training release and reserved for the challenge test; challenge test labels are not in the training download (confirm challenge rules before assuming a sealed evaluation).
10. **Overlap/contamination:** The 68 public center-5 cases reuse pre-cutoff INSTED (32 cases) and Lausanne TOF-MRA OpenNeuro (36 cases); TopCoW vessel labels and source images are pre-cutoff. New clinical center 1/2/4 images and the released aneurysm labels are the cleaner post-cutoff portion.
11. **Setups:** **S7/S8/S10/S22/S26**: train a vessel/aneurysm model at one center, test cross-center; use few (or no) target aneurysm annotations; or sequentially onboard an unseen site. G can edit probe/SAE-localized vascular-feature channels or add a rank-one location head; F fine-tunes/LoRA-trains on the same few labels. This is unusually relevant to aneurysm screening but small and class-imbalanced.

### 2. TopBrain whole-brain vessel anatomy release (new label extension; source images pre-cutoff)

1. **Name/links:** [Zenodo TopBrain Data Release, v3](https://zenodo.org/records/21972006); [TopBrain challenge](https://topbrain2026.grand-challenge.org/).
2. **Release date/evidence:** Zenodo version 3 published **2026-08-17**; record says “New in August 2026” and identifies the new TopAneu-compatible 36-class label release.
3. **Modality/anatomy:** MRA and CTA of intracranial vessels / Circle of Willis.
4. **Task/labels:** Multiclass voxel segmentation of 40 CTA / 42 MRA vessel anatomies in the v1 label maps, plus a modality-agnostic 36-class TopAneu v2 label map. Labels 1–12 and 15 are retained from 2025 TopCoW annotations; only the new label classes/annotation work should be counted as post-cutoff.
5. **Size:** **50 scans (25 CTA–MRA pairs)** in the released bundle; effectively 25 paired cases. Patient-level count is inferred from “25 pairs”; the record does not state an independent unique-person count beyond this.
6. **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** The release is derived from TopCoW batch-1/2 images; Zenodo description does not enumerate participating centers, scanner manufacturers, field strengths, or countries. Per-image modality and patient ID are encoded in filenames; no detailed acquisition/demographic metadata advertised.
7. **Temporal metadata:** CTA/MRA are paired, not longitudinal (no follow-up series stated).
8. **Access/license:** Open Zenodo download, but terms are **non-commercial use; commercial use requires permission; attribution required**.
9. **Test set:** This record is a training-data release; no hidden test split is defined in the Zenodo record. Do not treat its released labels as a sealed test set.
10. **Overlap/contamination:** All source scans were already public as TopCoW batches in 2025, and some labels were carried over from TopCoW. Post-cutoff novelty is the newly published expanded vessel annotation/label schema, not the image data. TopBrain labels also feed the organizer vessel predictions in TopAneu, so the two resources are not independent.
11. **Setups:** **S7/S8/S22/S26**: test representation transfer between MRA/CTA or to TopAneu’s new center data, with new vessel classes as target. Stronger as a small paired modality-shift / capability-extension benchmark than as an independent test cohort. Use 3D nnU-Net/UNETR or MRI foundation encoder; G edits probe-identified vessel channels/decoder logits, F LoRA/fine-tuning.

### 3. TotalSegmentator MRI dataset v3 (new version extension)

1. **Name/links:** [Zenodo v3 record](https://zenodo.org/records/22688334); [TotalSegmentator repository/model](https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator).
2. **Release date/evidence:** Zenodo API metadata gives `publication_date: 2026-09-10`, version 3.0.0, open dataset. Its description states v2 (616 images) was uploaded 2025-01-21 and v3 increased it to 1,296 images.
3. **Modality/anatomy:** Clinical whole-body and regional MRI; record says varied pathologies, scanners, sequences, and institutions, plus some Imaging Data Commons (IDC) scans.
4. **Task/labels:** Segmentation of **50 anatomical structures** (TotalSegmentator “total” task). Label provenance/degree of human review is not fully specified in the record; do not assume every mask is expert ground truth.
5. **Size:** v3 contains **1,296 MR images**; v2 had 616, so v3 adds **680 images**. The description itemizes 246 whole-body, 249 pediatric, and 170 previously failed cases (sum 665; the remaining 15 of the 680 increase are not reconciled in the record). Patient count is not stated; `meta.csv` carries source metadata.
6. **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** Heterogeneous clinical routine and IDC sources; number of sites/vendors/countries not reported in the landing record. Source metadata is available in `meta.csv`; acquisition/vendor/field-strength completeness needs inspection. No per-image demographic coverage summarized.
7. **Temporal metadata:** No longitudinal design described.
8. **Access/license:** Open Zenodo download under **CC BY 4.0**.
9. **Test set:** No hidden evaluation set; v3 images and labels are released together. Create patient- and source-disjoint sealed splits; the record does not claim a challenge split.
10. **Overlap/contamination:** The complete v3 archive carries the 616 pre-cutoff v2 images plus new images and improved labels on existing cases. IDC source images may have been public before cutoff. Strict study must isolate v3 additions using metadata and report the reused-image portion separately. Because this dataset trains the linked TotalSegmentator MRI model, checkpoint/dataset leakage is a particular concern.
11. **Setups:** **S6/S7/S25/S32**: old-version MRI segmenter → newly added whole-body/pediatric/difficult MRI; test target-domain generalization, few-shot new structures, or sparse updates. Use TotalSegmentator MRI v2 / 3D medical encoder. F uses standard LoRA/partial fine-tuning and augmentation; G adds probe-guided stem/normalization or decoder-logit edits. Feasible on one 24 GB GPU using 3D patches/sliding windows; large 3D full-volume training is not.

### 4. MAMA-MIAAI — “Here We Go Again” breast MRI extension

1. **Name/links:** [PhysioNet v1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/mama-miaai-herewegoagain/1.0.0/) (DOI [10.13026/w6wc-jh81](https://doi.org/10.13026/w6wc-jh81)); [MRI_BreastSeg model/code](https://github.com/FloXyPython/MRI_BreastSeg); [extension repository](https://github.com/FloXyPython/MAMA-MIAAI).
2. **Release date/evidence:** PhysioNet v1.0.0 is dated **2026-10-07** on the dataset record (the associated description/paper appeared in October 2026). This is an extension of the original MAMA-MIA, whose raw breast DCE-MRI data were public before the cutoff.
3. **Modality/anatomy:** Pre-treatment breast T1-weighted DCE-MRI, pre- and post-contrast time points; whole breast/fibroglandular tissue.
4. **Task/labels:** Whole-breast segmentation (94 manual masks plus automatic masks for all 1,506 cases) and bias-field-corrected image release. Not a new cancer outcome label set.
5. **Size:** **1,506 subjects/cases**, **1,506 bias-corrected DCE MRI volumes**, **7,868 image files/volumes as counted by the release description**, 1,506 automatic masks and 94 manual masks. Manual masks cover DUKE/ISPY1/ISPY2/NACT cohorts; 30 manual holdout cases are described (10 per cohort). Source describes 4 cohorts: DUKE 291, ISPY1 171, ISPY2 980, NACT 64 (these sum to 1,506).
6. **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** Four source cohorts and multi-center heterogeneous protocols; exact number of sites, vendors, countries, and per-patient demographic/scanner metadata are not enumerated in this extension page. Cohort and DCE timepoint are recoverable from identifiers/folder organization; original source metadata may be available from TCIA.
7. **Temporal metadata:** Within-exam DCE timing: pre-contrast and sequential post-contrast phases. It is not a longitudinal treatment-follow-up cohort in this extension.
8. **Access/license:** PhysioNet registration/access conditions; **CC BY-NC 4.0** for the adapted masks and bias-corrected data, consistent with source MAMA-MIA. Non-commercial only.
9. **Test set:** 94 manual annotations are all released, including cohort holdouts used for the segmentation model; automatic masks cover all cases. There is no hidden test set. Establish a new patient-level sealed split.
10. **Overlap/contamination:** Entire 1,506-case raw image collection was already public as MAMA-MIA. New after-cutoff information is the additional 94 manual masks, 1,506 auto masks and corrected images; do not call these novel raw MRI cases. Private cohorts A/B were used for model development but are not released.
11. **Setups:** **S6/S8/S14/S15**: DINOv3/natural-image encoder or MRI_BreastSeg source → heterogeneous breast DCE target; test low-label segmentation and the impact of the new correction/mask pipeline. G can edit probe-localized intensity/bias-field and breast-edge features while F fine-tunes/LoRA-trains. Manual holdout n≈30 is small; treat as pilot, not a powered definitive benchmark. A 24 GB GPU is sufficient for 3D U-Net patches and inference.

### 5. OpenBrain — auditable generated-label release for whole-brain MRI

1. **Name/links:** [Hugging Face dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lanqz7766/OpenBrain); [arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05348); [code](https://github.com/lanqz7766/openbrain).
2. **Release date/evidence:** Hugging Face API reports a public, ungated dataset, version 1.0, with `lastModified: 2026-11-06T02:29:10Z`; the arXiv manuscript is also November 2026. This is the first public HF snapshot located for the actual case records, not merely the paper.
3. **Modality/anatomy:** T1-weighted structural brain MRI; whole-brain parcellation.
4. **Task/labels:** 110-region generated parcellations (main evaluation uses unified 96-label space), two additional committee parcellations, brain masks, and seven per-case quality checks/risk fields. **These are model-generated, not manual labels.**
5. **Size:** **35,838 source-scoped T1w cases from 607 OpenNeuro datasets**. “Case” is a scan/session, not a deduplicated cross-dataset person; person count is not reported. One sample file per T1w scan plus label/QC records, with source archives distributed by dataset.
6. **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** 607 source datasets; actual physical-site/vendor/country totals are not given. Available age, sex, scanner/field-strength and health-status fields are reported when present, but coverage is incomplete/not imputed. Provenance/source dataset ID is included.
7. **Temporal metadata:** Source-scoped scan/session identifiers preserve some session information, but no harmonized longitudinal follow-up cohort is claimed.
8. **Access/license:** Public and ungated Hugging Face release; API card declares **CC0-1.0 / all data CC0** (source-specific terms/provenance should still be respected).
9. **Test set:** All release labels are distributed. Paper reports external evaluation cohorts (IXI, BrainLat, Mindboggle-101), but these are not a hidden test bundled with the release. Create a new source-dataset-disjoint test and keep any external manual labels sealed.
10. **Overlap/contamination:** Every raw scan is drawn from public OpenNeuro datasets, many available pre-cutoff; the release’s novelty is its generated labels, committee outputs and QC evidence. Existing pretrained models may have trained on overlapping public neuroimaging cohorts. The label generators and training provenance are known; their output is not a substitute for expert ground truth.
11. **Setups:** **S7/S25/S32/S39**: use QC-tiered generated parcellations as a large weak-label source, then hold out entire source cohorts/manual parcellation sets. Start from BrainFedFM/Brain2Vec or a 3D medical encoder. F trains on the same high-confidence/filtered subset with standard methods; G adds QC-/probe-guided sparse parcellation-logit or feature edits. Primary metric should be macro-Dice on sealed manual labels plus source-site and churn guardrails. One 24 GB GPU is feasible with cached features or 3D patches; full volume 35k fine-tuning needs a substantial time budget.

## Conditional post-cutoff candidates (do not count until data-file timestamps are checked)

### CFB-GBM v2.0

- **Links:** [MELBA v2.0 dataset article](https://www.melba-journal.org/papers/2026:038.html); [TCIA collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cfb-gbm/).
- **Date:** MELBA published **2026-09-21** and says v2.0 is publicly available on TCIA. However, the exact first TCIA upload/version timestamp for the new v2 annotations was not independently exposed in the sources inspected. Treat as **likely post-cutoff, but conditional** pending TCIA history.
- **Modality/anatomy/task:** Multiparametric brain MRI (T1 pre/post contrast, T2, FLAIR, T2*, ADC, etc.) plus planning CT/RTDOSE; glioblastoma GTV segmentation, radiomics, and derived RANO 2.0 response categories.
- **Size:** 264 adult GBM patients, longitudinal t0/t1/t2. v2 raises GTV delineation completion from 35% to 97%; brain masks, PyRadiomics features and all available t0→t1/t2 response pairs are added. The v2 paper does not state a single total image-file count.
- **Diversity/metadata:** One French oncology center; MRI Siemens 1.5T/3T (AREA/VIDA) and 16-channel coil. Per-patient clinical/treatment metadata, available modality/timepoint, MRI machine and time-difference-in-days are described. This is longitudinal, but not a multi-site vendor benchmark.
- **Access/test/overlap:** Public TCIA; collection-specific terms/license should be confirmed on TCIA (not specified in the v2 article). No hidden test; all new labels are public. Same 264 MRI cases were already in earlier CFB-GBM release (v1, pre-cutoff), so the new segmentation/RANO annotations—not raw images—are the eligible extension.
- **Setups:** If confirmed post-cutoff, useful for **S6/S9/S33** (serial post-treatment MRI, scanner/timepoint variation, label-protocol/RANO maintenance). Main limitation: one site and substantial old-image overlap.

### OpticNerveSeg (public-data-claim; artifact URL not verified)

- **Link:** [Scientific Data article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-08538-9).
- Article first published **2026-11-05** and describes a “public dataset” of 151 WU-Minn HCP subjects with masks of optic nerves, chiasm and tracts (binary segmentation). The raw HCP scans predate cutoff; the new annotation layer is the candidate extension.
- The article page/source content inspected did not surface a direct dataset-download endpoint, license/access terms, image count by sequence, or scanner/vendor metadata. Therefore it is **not counted as verified usable data** until the masks can be downloaded and a release record/date confirmed. If available, it could support S25 (adding a small structure) and few-shot anatomy transfer.

## Experimental setups enabled

Run all F/G comparisons from byte-identical checkpoints, equal labels/compute, and with edit-ablation plus random-edit controls. Sealed subject/source splits are required: many of these are public, inherited from older data, or release their labels.

1. **Breast DCE low-label segmentation and intensity-shift robustness (S14/S15; MAMA-MIAAI).** **Backbone:** DINOv3 ViT-B/ViT-L slice encoder or MRI_BreastSeg 3D U-Net; use the same released checkpoint for both arms. **Source → target:** original MAMA-MIA raw DCE / one cohort → other DCE cohorts or corrected-image variant; 10–30 manual masks for adaptation, 30 held-out manual masks for evaluation where split can be rebuilt. **F:** frozen/LoRA/full fine-tuning, identical augmentation and bias-field correction. **G:** F plus probe/SAE-derived breast-edge and bias-field feature rescaling or a sparse input-stem circuit; record changed tensors. **Metric:** Dice/HD95 on manual breast masks, plus source retention and prediction churn. **Practitioner value:** robust breast ROI preprocessing is a prerequisite for tumor/radiomic workflows. **24 GB:** yes with patch-based 3D U-Net or 2.5D; the manual test set is small, so treat as pilot.
2. **Brain tumor longitudinal maintenance and response tracking (S9/S33; conditional CFB-GBM v2.0).** **Backbone:** Brain2Vec encoder plus identical tumor decoder, or 3D nnU-Net. **Source → target:** pre-treatment t0 tumor delineations → post-treatment t1/t2 scans and newly completed v2 GTV/RANO labels; k=5/10/25 newly labeled patients. **F:** fine-tune/LoRA with standard intensity augmentation. **G:** F plus probe-derived progression/tumor feature direction and sparse output-head edits for the new volumetric RANO response criteria. **Metric:** tumor-region Dice/HD95; RANO response agreement; t0 retained Dice and churn. **Practitioner value:** earlier, consistent response monitoring could shorten time to therapy changes. **24 GB:** yes with patch/crop training and sliding-window inference. **Eligibility caveat:** use only if TCIA shows v2 labels first posted after cutoff.
3. **Aneurysm center onboarding / rare-location transfer (S7/S8/S22/S26; TopAneu + TopBrain).** **Backbone:** 3D nnU-Net / SwinUNETR vessel model pretrained on TopBrain vessel maps; treat the TopAneu vessel masks as silver labels and lesion masks as target truth. **Source → target:** TopBrain/TopCoW vessel anatomy or one TopAneu center → newly released private center-1/2/4 TopAneu scans; k=0/5/10 target-labeled cases, with UMC Utrecht sealed. **F:** standard LoRA/fine-tuning and intensity augmentation on same data. **G:** F plus probe-localized vessel-feature rescaling/ablation and sparse aneurysm-location head edits from same few cases. **Metric:** aneurysm lesion-wise F1/FROC and location/type macro-F1; vessel Dice is secondary. **Practitioner value:** new-center aneurysm detection with rare anatomical classes and no broad relabeling. **24 GB:** likely yes using cropped 3D volumes; full-volume multi-class training could be tight. **Caveat:** do not use the 68 recycled public scans as “new” test data; TopBrain and TopAneu overlap.
4. **Field-strength upgrade: paired 0.1T–7T MRI synthesis (S6/S12/S49; MRIxFields2026, borderline).** **Backbone:** a 3D brain MRI encoder (BrainFedFM/Brain2Vec) feeding a shared conditional 3D U-Net; use MRIxFields’ travelling-volunteer pairs. **Source → target:** 0.1/1.5/3/5T → 7T; evaluate all modality contrasts; unpaired retrospective scans are a secondary regime. **F:** paired supervised training/LoRA, standard image losses and augmentation. **G:** F plus field-strength-probe-guided stem/normalization edits and a sparse field-conditioned circuit derived from paired differences. **Metric:** PSNR/SSIM/NMSE plus SynthSeg tissue/structure Dice and anatomical consistency; guard against hallucinated detail. **Practitioner value:** addresses low-/portable-field quality and cross-scanner upgrades. **24 GB:** yes with 2D/2.5D or 3D patches; full volumes should be tiled. **Release:** April 2026, so borderline only.
5. **Model-version and pediatric/domain generalization for anatomical MRI segmentation (S6/S7/S25/S32; TotalSegmentator MRI v3).** **Backbone:** TotalSegmentator MRI v2 checkpoint or 3D medical encoder. **Source → target:** pre-v3 616-image set → v3-only new whole-body/pediatric/difficult cases; isolate non-duplicated source IDs and use new labels only. **F:** fine-tune/LoRA with standard resampling and intensity augmentation. **G:** F plus interpretability/probe-guided stem normalization or sparse decoder-logit edits; measure edit contribution and source retention. **Metric:** 50-structure Dice/NSD and worst-source/pediatric Dice. **Practitioner value:** assess adaptation when a model meets pediatric anatomy or new MR regions it previously missed. **24 GB:** yes with 3D patches. **Caveats:** release itemization misses 15 cases; exact annotation provenance and site/vendor metadata need checking; v2 images are pre-cutoff.
6. **Weak-label quality-aware whole-brain segmentation (S7/S25/S32; OpenBrain).** **Backbone:** BrainFedFM or Brain2Vec 3D encoder with a common parcellation decoder. **Source → target:** high-confidence OpenBrain generated-label tiers from many OpenNeuro sources → entirely held-out source datasets and a sealed manual-label cohort; use 100/500/1,000 training cases. **F:** QC-tier-matched standard supervised fine-tuning. **G:** F plus QC/probe-derived sparse ROI-specific feature or logit edits; include all-edit and per-edit reversions and a random-edit baseline. **Metric:** sealed manual macro-Dice (especially small ROIs), worst-source Dice, calibration and churn. **Practitioner value:** can the large volume of auditable pseudo-labels reduce segmentation annotation costs without importing bad masks? **24 GB:** yes using cached features/patch sampling; all 35k full-volume end-to-end training may exceed a practical single-GPU budget.

## Relevant pretrained backbones

**Post-cutoff releases (mark explicitly):**

- **BrainFedFM** — structural 3D brain MRI foundation model; paper [arXiv 2609.27611](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27611), code and pretrained weights [GitHub](https://github.com/SPIresearch/BrainFedFM). Reported federated pretraining on 164,707 T1w scans across 42 sites. **Released after cutoff (2026; arXiv/repository source).** Strong source for S6/S7, but check whether the exact checkpoint already used any target cohorts.
- **Brain2Vec** — self-supervised 3D MAE on 74,425 T1w scans from iSTAGING; [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28411), [GitHub](https://github.com/CBICA/Brain2Vec), [Hugging Face weights](https://huggingface.co/cbica/Brain2Vec). 768-D frozen embedding or ViT initialization. **Released after cutoff (October 2026); open weights are stated by the project.** Its pretraining cohort contains MCI/dementia and broad study overlap; audit contamination before clinical tests.
- **MRI_BreastSeg** — specialist 3D U-Net, weights/code linked from the 2026 MAMA-MIAAI extension ([GitHub](https://github.com/FloXyPython/MRI_BreastSeg)). **Post-cutoff specialist checkpoint, not a general foundation model.** Useful baseline/upper bound for breast whole-tissue segmentation, but trained on source/private development data, so do not evaluate on its known training/holdout masks as independent evidence.

**Established/pre-cutoff controls:**

- **DINOv3** ([Meta repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3), 2025 release): natural-image ViT backbone for the preferred natural-image→MRI domain-gap studies (2D/2.5D slices; not MRI-pretrained).
- **SynthSeg/SynthSeg+** ([FreeSurfer/SynthSeg](https://github.com/BBillot/SynthSeg), 2023): robust MRI segmentation baseline across contrast/resolution; not an FM, but a relevant mechanical/clinical baseline.
- **FOMO25/AMAES and OpenMind-derived MRI encoders** (pre-cutoff models; [FOMO-MRI collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/FOMO-MRI/fomo-mri-datasets)): suitable 3D MRI pretraining baselines. Check individual checkpoint cards for data/DUA and training-source overlap. FOMO300K itself is borderline (Jan–Apr 2026 release), not a post-cutoff dataset.
- **Brain-SAM** ([medRxiv, Jan 2026](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.30.26345164v2)): 3D SAM2-derived brain MRI lesion segmentation proposal/checkpoint family, **before cutoff**; verify open weight availability before selecting it.
- **TotalSegmentator** ([GitHub](https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator)): established segmentation model family; v3 MRI dataset released post-cutoff, but the precise date/version of any separately distributed v3 model checkpoint should be checked before calling the weights post-cutoff.

## Contradictions and missing evidence

- **No substantive contradictions found** on the confirmed release dates. A recurring false positive is paper date ≠ dataset date: BreastDCEDL appeared as a 2026 paper but its cited Zenodo dataset version was deposited in 2025; AMYPAD’s 2026 descriptor uses v202306 data; FOMO300K scans were available by early 2026.
- **Source-check validation limitation:** the requested `source_check` call for the TotalSegmentator v3 cutoff claim returned “missing-evidence” because Exa hit its free 429 rate limit. I inspected the official Zenodo record and its API metadata directly; the page/API explicitly reports v3 date 2026-09-10, version 3.0.0, CC BY 4.0, prior v2 date, and image-count change.
- **Residual release-date uncertainty:** exact upload times for CFB-GBM v2 and OpticNerveSeg masks were not independently located; neither is counted as a confirmed eligible release. MosaicMRI’s first data-file publication is unclear. PiCaRad’s cited Zenodo record provided a paper PDF rather than the feature matrices. The release-rule call should be revisited if archive version histories expose dates.
- **Residual research risks:** most “new” data are annotation/metadata extensions on old scans; public-source duplication, hidden overlap between pretraining corpora and test cohorts, automatically generated labels, small manual-mask test sets, and inconsistent vendor/site metadata are substantial. Patient-level plus near-duplicate/source-level deduplication and per-image metadata audit are prerequisites.

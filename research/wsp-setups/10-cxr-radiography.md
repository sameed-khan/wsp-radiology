# Research: Post-cutoff chest X-ray and projection-radiography datasets

**Scope/date checked:** projection radiography, especially CXR, portable/bedside CXR, MSK/fracture and dental/panoramic X-ray. Cutoff is **2026-07-01** (inclusive); research snapshot **2026-11-12**. I treated a repository's actual data-file publication/first-version date as the release date, not the paper date. “Eligible” below means a new image collection or a distinct post-cutoff annotation/linkage/synthetic-data release. Old source images re-bundled in a new benchmark are not considered new data. Access rights refer to the dataset itself, not merely the record metadata.

## Summary

The strongest newly available real-image resources in this shard are **Naija-CXR** (Nigerian population; unusually high relevance to geographic transfer, though its public documentation omits cohort counts), **XQA-Chest** (paired expert image/report references plus controlled IQA degradations), and the newly released **dental OPG collections** (MOPG-7, DentalOPG-1550; plus restricted BoneLoss-PAN769). The largest bedside/temporal CXR cohort, **TAIX-Ray**, is a valuable distribution-shift dataset but its image files were already uploaded in February 2026, so it is *borderline*, not eligible under the July cutoff. Several large new post-cutoff CXR releases are annotation/linkage extensions of MIMIC or older multicentre CXR sources; they are useful but carry substantial overlap/contamination risk. I found no well-documented, newly released multi-country raw X-ray cohort after the cutoff; MOPG-7 is multi-clinic but all sites are in Bangladesh.

## Eligible: publicly released data or a genuinely new post-cutoff extension

### 1. Naija-CXR — Nigerian chest radiographs and reports
- **Links:** [Zenodo record (v1.1)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21781842); [API metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/21781842). **Release date/evidence:** Zenodo API says `publication_date: 2026-08-04`, `created: 2026-08-03`; the image archive `RSFUTH_CXR.zip` is attached. Thus the *data files*, not merely a paper, were available post-cutoff. **Confidence: high** on date.
- **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** de-identified frontal CXR, reportedly paired with radiologist reports and expert annotations; classification/report generation/VLM research. The landing page says label definitions are provided but does not specify the exact label ontology, annotation granularity, or counts.
- **Size:** not stated in public record text; one 389.3-MB ZIP. Patient/image counts **unverified**.
- **Sites, devices, countries, metadata:** Nigerian hospital sources; Zenodo creators include Rasheed Shekoni Federal University Teaching Hospital, Dutse. Exact site count/device/vendor and per-image site/device fields are not documented. It is likely a Nigerian geographic-domain target; do not assume more than one imaging site from “institutions” in the description. Demographic/clinical metadata are described as available “where available,” not enumerated.
- **Temporal:** no acquisition dates/period reported in the repository record.
- **Access/license:** open downloadable files; Zenodo metadata says **CC BY 4.0**, while record prose limits intended use to **non-commercial scientific research/education**. This is a license/terms inconsistency—obtain depositor clarification and follow the more restrictive language pending that clarification.
- **Test set:** no official split or hidden labels documented; labels appear to be released with the dataset. A new patient-level split and duplicate audit are required.
- **Overlap:** no source overlap stated. Check perceptual/embedding duplicates against public CXR sets and any Nigerian benchmark before claiming a clean external test.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S1/S7/S52** (best new real-world candidate): CheXpert/MIMIC-trained RadDINO or CXR-FM → Nigerian CXR, R0–R2. Use only labels/target covariates actually supplied; if the archive lacks a usable expert label schema, first verify it. Potential domain shift includes population, disease prevalence and acquisition, not just scanner style.

### 2. XQA-Chest — expert reference standards and image-quality ratings
- **Links:** [UK Data Service/ReShare record](https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/858610/); [data README](https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/858610/11/README.md); [dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.5255/UKDA-SN-858610); [paper/preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26333). **Release date/evidence:** repository record says **2026-08-26**; README says the collection is under the ReShare safeguarded-access route. The paper is also post-cutoff, but the record date is the release evidence. **Confidence: high.**
- **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** frontal CXR (AP/PA). XQA-RR: 14 CheXpert/MIMIC-style finding categories, independent image-derived and report-derived expert labels (negative/uncertain/positive; report additionally “not mentioned”), plus optional positive-finding boxes. XQA-IQA: expert ratings of diagnostic usability for original and controlled degraded images (contrast/sharpness), 4-point scale.
- **Size:** XQA-RR: **650 studies/images with paired reports** from Cambridge University Hospitals (CUH); XQA-IQA: **323 reference CXRs + 1,571 degraded variants**. The accompanying paper additionally describes **735 re-annotated MIMIC-CXR studies**; confirm these files are included in the approved download before treating that subset as part of the released collection. Patient count is not separately stated.
- **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** primary collection is one UK trust; the paper’s MIMIC subset is a separate US source. Exact scanner/vendor counts and demographic/site fields are not exposed in the README; Cambridge acquisition dates are 2019–2021 and study-level duplicate encounters excluded.
- **Temporal:** Cambridge studies 2019–2021; dates/locations are removed from the released metadata. No longitudinal follow-up guarantee.
- **Access/license:** safeguarded access through UK Data Service; approved user registration, depositor permission/data-access review and UK Data Service End User Licence. Not open-download.
- **Test set:** annotations/labels are released to approved users; no hidden challenge test. Small cohort is not adequately powered for ≤0.01 AUROC changes; use as a mechanistic/annotation-reference study, not as the sole large confirmatory test.
- **Overlap:** Cambridge images are new; possible link/overlap of the additional 735-study subset is with pre-cutoff MIMIC-CXR. Keep it separate. Finding references are new expert annotations, not a clean post-cutoff image collection.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S51 / S13-null / S8**: compare the same fixed CXR-FM or MedImageInsight checkpoint and few-shot fine-tuning against interpretability-derived local edits under alternative expert-image vs. report references; IQA subset can test an image-quality adaptation. Score AUROC/reference sensitivity, Kendall rank of model outcomes, and expert-IQA agreement. Treat as underpowered pilot.

### 3. CheXmask-U — uncertainty-aware organ masks across five CXR datasets
- **Links:** [PhysioNet dataset v1.0.0](https://www.physionet.org/content/chexmask-u/1.0.0/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.13026/6vn3-3j51); [paper/preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10715). **Release date/evidence:** PhysioNet labels version 1.0.0 as an initial release in **September 2026** (record page gives Sep 2, 2026); release notes say initial release. The preprint was earlier, but the release date used here is the repository data date. **Confidence: high.**
- **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** CXR-derived **segmentation** masks and anatomical landmarks/uncertainty for left lung, right lung and heart; uncertainty estimation/calibration/OOD/quality control, not disease labels.
- **Size:** annotations for **657,566 images**: ChestX-ray8 112,120; CheXpert 187,825; MIMIC-CXR-JPG 243,334; PadChest 96,287; VinDr-CXR 18,000. It releases IDs, landmark means/stds, RLE masks and RCA quality estimates; **it does not contain the images**.
- **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** inherited from the five parent datasets: US (NIH, CheXpert, MIMIC), Spain (PadChest), Vietnam (VinDr); exact center/scanner/vendor cardinality and metadata are in parent records, not consolidated here. Input/target provenance per image is keyed by parent dataset ID. The uncertainty model itself was trained on 911 Chest-Xray-Landmark images, including JSRT, PadChest, Montgomery and Shenzhen, with no held-out model-training split.
- **Temporal:** no new acquisition dates; inherits parent-dataset dates/metadata.
- **Access/license:** annotation files are on PhysioNet; the page does not identify original images as included. Source images must be obtained separately under each source dataset’s conditions, including credentialed/DUA access for MIMIC. Dataset-specific license not established from the retrieved description—check record metadata before redistribution.
- **Test set:** no hidden test; image IDs/masks and quality labels are public to users with source-image access. RCA is a mask-quality proxy, not independent pixel-level truth.
- **Overlap:** **complete image-level overlap** with five public, pre-cutoff datasets, and the supplied mask model was trained on overlapping source collections. This is an annotation/uncertainty extension only; cannot function as a fresh image-generalization test without a separate new cohort.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S8/S11/S32/S41** for uncertainty-aware adaptation, low-churn mask calibration,/selective prediction or mask quality screening; use only a *different*, sealed target set (e.g. Naija-CXR) for target evaluation. Not suitable as test-only fresh data.

### 4. MIMIC-Ext-CXR-QBA — structured CXR VQA, localized question/answer pairs
- **Links:** [PhysioNet v1.0.1](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-ext-cxr-qba/1.0.1/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.13026/hne5-7t85); [ICLR 2026 paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=LrmyW9JLYq). **Release date/evidence:** PhysioNet lists the data as published **Aug. 25, 2026** (v1.0.1); versioned dataset archive, not just the paper. **Confidence: high** that the current release is post-cutoff; version-history check for the exact first v1.0.0 day was not necessary to the cutoff distinction because it is the same late-August release family.
- **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** MIMIC frontal/lateral chest X-rays with report-derived scene graphs, structured findings/regions, bounding boxes, and full-sentence hierarchical VQA. Classification/detection/localization/report/VQA targets; 310 possible region types, 237 finding entities described.
- **Size:** **42,172,827 QA pairs**, generated from **227,827 MIMIC-CXR reports** and the MIMIC-CXR-JPG pool of about **377,110 images**. Quality-selected subsets: 31,230,906 pretraining pairs and 7,532,281 fine-tuning pairs. Patient count follows MIMIC-CXR (about 65K; use parent source for exact cohort definition). Images themselves are not re-released.
- **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** one US hospital system (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston); no multi-site/vendor diversity. Existing MIMIC image identifiers, view and patient-level metadata are inherited; no new vendor labels claimed.
- **Temporal:** inherits MIMIC-CXR acquisition years, approximately 2011–2016; source report temporal mentions are extracted into scene-graph fields. No new temporal image cohort.
- **Access/license:** PhysioNet credentialing, CITI training and DUA; annotation package alone still requires source MIMIC-CXR image access. Follow PhysioNet/MIMIC terms rather than assume a permissive standalone license.
- **Test set:** train/validation/test or task split details should be taken from the published files; labels/QA are not a hidden challenge set. Avoid same-patient/study leakage and uncritically treating automatic answers as gold.
- **Overlap:** wholly derived from pre-cutoff MIMIC-CXR/JPG, reports and earlier anatomy/localization data (including Chest ImaGenome/CXAS); high study/model contamination risk despite the post-cutoff annotations.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S24/S47/S17**: add a structured VQA/localization capability to a generalist medical VLM or CXR FM with a small sealed target QA set; F = prompt/LoRA/fine-tuning, G = F plus probe/SAE-derived edits in image/text projection or answer heads. Evaluate answer factuality and box/localization, retained common diagnosis performance and churn. Use it as training data, not a novel held-out image test.

### 5. MIMIC-CXR-DB — new admission/EHR linkage for existing MIMIC radiographs
- **Links:** [PhysioNet v1.0](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-cxr-ext-mimic-cxr-db/1.0/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.13026/gy0s-e371). **Release date/evidence:** PhysioNet reports **Oct. 1, 2026**, initial release; this is a new data linkage/metadata release, not new X-ray acquisition. **Confidence: high.**
- **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** CXR image IDs/metadata plus report-derived findings, linked to admission diagnoses, demographics, labs and discharge/radiology-note concepts. Supports multimodal clinical-outcome/classification tasks rather than new pixel annotation.
- **Size:** 377,095 radiographs considered; 227,827 studies; **146,333 images** linked to inpatient admission windows. One study can contain multiple radiographs. Patient count inherited from MIMIC; exact linked unique-patient count not stated in the record.
- **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** one Boston hospital / MIMIC source. Added metadata includes dates/times, view position, DICOM dimensions/procedure fields, demographics and admission/diagnosis fields. Site/device metadata remain essentially single-source.
- **Temporal:** MIMIC study, admission, discharge and acquisition timestamps are included, under original MIMIC date shifting/de-identification; provides admission-window linkage.
- **Access/license:** PhysioNet credentialed access and DUA; users cannot redistribute or reidentify; code-sharing obligations noted. Not open-download.
- **Test set:** no sealed challenge test; full linkage tables are released. Labels partly derived by NLP and NER/ontology linking; authors explicitly caution that individual extracted claims were not expert-validated, and they report failures to fully link some images to admissions/reports.
- **Overlap:** all radiographs are pre-cutoff MIMIC-CXR/JPG; newly available part is linkage/context. Never count as a fresh image test set.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S9/S13/S53** with a fixed MIMIC checkpoint and restricted-data/statistics adaptation; exploit clinical covariates for label shift/outcome analyses. For a real WSP comparison, keep all data-access budgets matched and validate extraction noise.

### 6. MOPG-7 — multi-clinic dental panoramic radiographs
- **Links:** [Mendeley Data v1 (first version)](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/t66ytdrhf2/1); [v2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/t66ytdrhf2/2). **Release date/evidence:** version history shows v1 published **Sep. 16, 2026** (v2 Sep. 17); files and annotation description are directly on Mendeley. **Confidence: high.**
- **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** panoramic dental OPG; object detection/localization in YOLO and COCO formats; seven categories: missing teeth, crown, root canal, caries, wisdom teeth, broken-down teeth, healthy OPG.
- **Size:** **2,095 images** from four centers; **9,834 expert-validated boxes** after removing 127 poor boxes. Patient count and patient overlap across images are not stated. Center counts: 1,433, 533, 114 and 15 images.
- **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** four dental centers in **Bangladesh** (Sonia Nursing Home/Tangail; Ibn Sina/Dayaganj; Niramoy/Tangail; Health City/Gaibandha). Scanner vendor/device mix not reported. `image_metadata.csv` and `split_assignments.csv` are included; verify which patient/site/demographic fields they actually contain before using them for holdout splits.
- **Temporal:** acquisition dates not reported in public description; ethics approval Dec. 2, 2025 is not image-acquisition date.
- **Access/license:** public Mendeley download; specific dataset license not apparent on the fetched record, so do not assume commercial reuse rights.
- **Test set:** train/validation/test splits released (exact split counts should be read from metadata). Labels are visible; not a hidden challenge. Confirm splits are patient-level before scoring.
- **Overlap:** no named parent dataset; potentially related to other recent Bangladesh dental releases (including DentalOPG-1550) from different/unknown centers. Deduplicate by perceptual hashes/embeddings.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S1/S7/S14/S26**: train on some clinics, hold out an entire clinic or another OPG center; use ImageNet/DINOv3 or dental FM. F = detector fine-tuning/augmentation; G = same plus probe-guided sparse feature/normalization edits. Primary mAP50–95/worst-clinic recall; feasible with 24 GB GPU.

### 7. DentalOPG-1550 — dental disease detection on panoramic images
- **Links:** [Mendeley v1 (raw collection)](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/rtt726b26d/1); [v2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/rtt726b26d/2); [v3](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/rtt726b26d/3). **Release date/evidence:** v1 of the image collection published **Sep. 1, 2026**; the annotated detection release appeared v2 on Sep. 29 and expanded v3 on Oct. 2. - **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** panoramic dental radiographs; v3 provides YOLOv8 boxes for 14 categories, including caries, periodontitis, apical abscess, impactions, restorations, teeth and normal teeth.
- **Size:** **1,550 raw OPGs**; v3 annotated subset **1,525 images**, **14,497 objects** (train/val/test: 1,068/303/154 images; object counts 10,141/2,934/1,422). A v2 record reported a different count (**10,141** boxes total), so use v3 and flag version change. Patients not counted.
- **Sites/vendors/countries/metadata:** one diagnostic centre; exact name/site/country/device not specified in v3 record. Annotation reviewer is based in Bogura, Bangladesh, which is not proof the images were acquired there. No vendor or per-image patient/site/demographic metadata verified.
- **Temporal:** no image acquisition period provided.
- **Access/license:** Mendeley data records public; dataset license not stated in retrieved record. V3 images were auto-oriented, stretched to 640×640 RGB JPEG; confirm whether raw image downloads coexist before assuming original geometry.
- **Test set:** released split labels, no hidden challenge test; the 154-image test is small and not enough for precise subgroup effects.
- **Overlap:** no published source parent; overlap with recent local dental releases remains unknown; dedupe with MOPG-7.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S10/S14/S22**: localized few-shot detection in a sparse, long-tailed dental taxonomy; start from DINOv3/ImageNet and optionally compare with dental-specific encoders. F = few-shot YOLO/LoRA; G = F plus interpretation-guided edits to lesion-sensitive features. mAP50–95 and per-class recall; 24-GB feasible at 640px.

### 10. CompDiff synthetic CXR corpora — natural and demographically balanced releases
- **Links:** [natural 62,094-image corpus](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23339629); [balanced 415,072-image corpus](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23339643); [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16551); [model weights](https://huggingface.co/mahmoudibra98/compdiff-chest-xray). **Release date/evidence:** records show **Oct. 27, 2026**, with image parquet files attached; both are post-cutoff synthetic releases. **Confidence: high.**
- **Modality/anatomy/tasks:** synthetic PA chest radiographs, 512×512 PNG embedded in parquet. Each has a de-identified report prompt and 14 CheXpert labels; demographic conditioning (sex, race, age group). 62,094 natural-distribution one-per-impression images; 415,072 balanced/intersectional set generated by expanding source impressions across demographic combinations. These labels are inherited conditioning, not independent annotations of synthetic images.
- **Sites/scanners/countries/metadata:** no real acquisition sites/vendors for generated images. Generator trained on MIMIC-CXR; each row includes prompt and sex/race/age group, labels, and generator metadata. Source patients/data not new.
- **Temporal:** follows source MIMIC records; synthetic generation date is 2026. No new clinical time series.
- **Access/license:** open Zenodo archives (18 GB and 121.9 GB); precise license/redistribution terms need confirmation from record README and MIMIC terms. Prompt text originates from MIMIC and inherits its governance constraints.
- **Test set:** training corpora, no held-out clinical test. Do not use conditioning labels as image ground truth or score external performance on these generated training samples.
- **Overlap:** source reports/labels are directly from MIMIC-CXR pre-cutoff; generative model trained on MIMIC. High memorization and source-label overlap risk; use as augmentation/audit only.
- **WSP setup fit:** **S28/S32** (representation fairness/attribute inference, robustness under controlled conditioned generations) with a real sealed set as evaluation. F = standard augmentation/retraining; G = interpretability-guided feature edits. 24 GB possible for classifier fine-tuning on subsets; full balanced corpus is 121.9 GB and needs streaming/storage.

- **Prompt2SegCXR:** [Mendeley v1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/mk36vt2nzj/1) was published **Dec. 27, 2024**; v2 is Aug. 7, 2026 but describes the same doodle prompts/masks over old JSRT, VinDr-RibCXR and Shenzhen images, with no documented new post-cutoff cohort/annotation increment. Not eligible unless a separately new v2 component can be isolated.
- **ChestX6:** [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21880301), Aug. 11, 2026, 18,036 images combined from four already-public CXR datasets. A fresh packaging/label balance is not new image data; exclude unless authors show genuinely new annotation content.
- **CXR-LT 2026 challenge:** multi-center challenge paper/data use PadChest and NIH ChestX-ray14; paper appeared before cutoff and source images are old. Do not count a 2026 challenge date as first image availability.
- **PediURF / pediatric radius-ulna fractures:** [Figshare](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/PediURF_A_Comprehensive_X-ray_Dataset_for_The_Pediatric_Ulna_and_Radius_Fractures_Analysis/29998954) v1 was posted Aug. 28, 2025 (v2 Nov. 2025), despite the Scientific Data article in Jan. 2026—outside even the borderline window.
- **PediaSHF-DX, pediatric supracondylar humerus fractures:** [Figshare](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/A_Benchmark_X-ray_Dataset_for_Pediatric_Supracondylar_Humerus_Fracture_with_Improved_YOLOv11-Based_Detection/29322869) version history shows first data v1 June 15, 2025 (v2 Jul. 4, 2025); 10,325 images/5,163 patients reported in the 2026 paper. The paper's March 2026 date does not qualify the data.
- **X-Pan:** [Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22838356) describes 1,566 OPGs and is dated Sep. 28, 2026, but the inspected Zenodo record contains only a PDF paper, not the image collection. Paper says complete materials at a project repository, which was not verified. Do not count as an available dataset until direct image download/access is confirmed.
- **HarMoE multi-source CXR pretraining:** [MM ’26 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02252) is a potentially important post-cutoff *model* using 873K old images (MIMIC-CXR, CheXpert, ChestX-ray14, PadChest), but its paper says code and harmonized dataset “will be released”; no checkpoint/data release was verified here. It does not create new source radiographs.
- Several broad 2026 papers/collections and small Zenodo CXR uploads were surfaced but either repackage legacy datasets, lack a traceable cohort/label provenance, or supply no imaging files; they are not promoted as validated datasets. Search-result release dates alone were not treated as evidence.

For large MIMIC-derived releases, mark the new annotations as post-cutoff but the underlying image/report content as pre-cutoff.

## Contradictions / disputed points

- **Naija-CXR reuse terms:** Zenodo says CC BY 4.0 in machine-readable metadata, but prose says non-commercial scientific/education only. Resolve before use or redistribution.
- **DentalOPG-1550 label versions:** v2 reported 10,141 boxes; v3 reports 14,497 total across 1,525 annotated images while retaining a train split with 10,141. Use v3 and do not mix version counts.
- **BoneLoss-PAN769:** record metadata advertises CC BY 4.0 but marks data restricted and attaches no image files; usable access/licensing is unresolved.
- **TAIX-Ray:** the paper is post-cutoff but the actual HF uploads are Feb. 2026. This is exactly why a paper’s publication date cannot establish data eligibility.
- **XQA-Chest:** the paper discusses an additional 735-image MIMIC subset; the public README clearly documents the 650-image CUH cohort and IQA subset. Confirm whether the MIMIC labels/images are included in the safeguarded download.

## Missing evidence / residual risks

1. Naija-CXR image/patient counts, diagnosis label schema, split, exact number of institutions, acquisition period and scanner metadata are not disclosed in the record text; inspect ZIP/README and request depositor clarification before designing a powered study.
2. Small XQA/dental datasets are not automatically patient-independent or sufficiently powered; duplicate audit and split audit are prerequisites.
3. Dataset-specific commercial licenses for MOPG-7 and DentalOPG-1550 were not identified; do not infer rights from the repository being downloadable.
4. CheXmask-U’s original image files are absent and its annotation generator was trained on a source mix with overlap. Treat its RCA/uncertainty as an auxiliary resource, not independent gold truth.
5. MIMIC-QBA/ILS/DB add late public annotations/metadata but use pre-cutoff images and reports; any experiment targeting model non-memorization must exclude those sources from prior training and prohibit public test-set tuning.
6. The broad sweep located more preprints, repository updates and derivative compilations than could be individually verified. Exa free-tier rate limiting blocked some follow-up searches; remaining repository leads (especially DMCH-PneumoX, FaceBase panoramic radiographs and X-Pan) should be checked directly for image-file availability and license.

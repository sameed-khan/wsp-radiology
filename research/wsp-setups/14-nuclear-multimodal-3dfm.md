# Research: Nuclear medicine, intervention, multimodal imaging, and 3D foundation-model data (cutoff 2026-07-01)

**Scope and method.** Reviewed new PET/hybrid-imaging and interventional releases, multimodal imaging extensions, and recent 3D benchmark/pretraining assets. Search-tool substitution: the brief specifies `exa_search` and forbids `web_search`, but this session exposed no `exa_search`; following the supervisor’s instruction, I used the available search tool with `provider: exa`, then inspected original TCIA/IDC, Zenodo, Hugging Face, arXiv, GitHub, TAIR/AWS and institutional pages directly. Exa’s free rate limit blocked additional searches after the first batch, so the later sweep relied on direct source fetching and source-linked leads. Findings below distinguish an actual data release from a paper announcement or new version of old data.

## Summary

The strongest cutoff-eligible resources are (1) an October 2026 additive release of new radiology imaging in ten Cancer Moonshot Biobank (CMB) collections, including PET/NM and angiography XA in some cohorts; (2) TAIR’s November 2026 cerebrovascular CTA/MRA and video-fluoroscopy release, with a release-status contradiction for fluoroscopy and annotations; and (3) the November 2026 HoloCT model/HoloDATA release, which is a useful new 3D foundation-model/split-and-annotation suite but **not** a new 195,921-scan raw-image corpus. MetaStructAtlas is particularly promising for whole-body PET/CT, but as of this check only three preview question records were actually available; the full 490-patient dataset is announced, not released. No cutoff-eligible FAP-tracer dataset or complete new image-plus-EHR cohort was verified.

## Findings

### 1. Cutoff-eligible releases and extensions (2026-07-01 onward)

#### A. Cancer Moonshot Biobank (CMB) radiology additions — new post-cutoff images in legacy cohorts

- **Name and links:** [IDC V25 release notes (October 2026)](https://learn.canceridc.dev/data/data-release-notes), [TCIA CMB imaging program](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/research/cmb/), and collection links: [CMB-LCA](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-lca/), [CMB-CRC](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-crc/), [CMB-BRCA](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-brca/), [CMB-MML](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-mml/), [CMB-PCA](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-pca/), [CMB-MEL](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-mel/), [CMB-GEC](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-gec/), [CMB-OV](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-ov/), [CMB-AML](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmb-aml/), and [Vestibular-Schwannoma-MC-RC](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/vestibular-schwannoma-mc-rc/).
- **Release date/evidence:** October 2026 IDC V25. The IDC release notes say TCIA published new collection versions adding radiology and that the additions were ingested into IDC in V25; they explicitly compare the new UIDs against existing IDC and report no overlap. This is an eligible **new-image extension** of pre-existing cohorts, not a newly recruited cohort.
- **Modality/anatomy; task/labels:** Mixed oncologic radiology, including CT, DX, MR, PET (PT), nuclear medicine (NM), ultrasound, and XA; organ/system depends on collection. These are clinical imaging cohorts, not a new harmonized segmentation/challenge benchmark. The imaging release notes do not describe a new image-level label set. Cohort-level cancer diagnosis and related phenotypic/clinical/genomic records exist for the CMB program; for CMB-LCA, associated clinical and biospecimen data are said to be hosted in dbGaP/NCI repositories, with separate access conditions. **Do not assume** that individual labels are linked to each newly added series without checking collection metadata.
- **Size:** V25 adds **246 patients with new series, 3,512 series and 401,190 image instances** across ten collections. Of the 246, 84 are new to IDC; 65 already had pathology images and now gain radiology; 97 gain further studies. PET/XA/NM-relevant subcollections are listed below (all counts are added series/instances, not whole-collection totals):

  | Collection | Patients with new series (patient delta) | Added series / instances | Modalities listed for added radiology |

  The counts sum to the release-note totals. CMB-LCA’s TCIA/IDC collection page describes the overall CMB initiative as collecting longitudinal data across at least ten cancer types and multiple NCORP sites; it does **not** establish that every collection is represented at every site.
- **Sites/vendors/countries and metadata:** CMB is a U.S. multi-site NCI program, with multiple NCORP sites; exact number of contributing centers for each subcollection, scanner/vendor mix, and image-level site/device/demographic fields are not given in V25’s change log. DICOM headers and linked collection metadata need separate audit.
- **Temporal metadata:** The CMB program is longitudinal, but the V25 note does not specify acquisition dates/timepoints for each newly added study. Verify dates and linkage in TCIA metadata before temporal-split experiments.
- **Access/license:** Imaging is available through TCIA/IDC; IDC states that the radiology additions match TCIA’s **CC BY 4.0** downloads. Associated clinical/genomic tables may be separate and credentialed through dbGaP/NCI; do not treat the full EHR/clinical record as openly downloadable with the images.
- **Test-set situation:** Public cohort data, no hidden challenge test set described. Create patient-level sealed splits, deduplicate across the pre-existing cohort and the new additions, and distinguish same-patient pathology from new radiology.
- **Pre-cutoff overlap/contamination:** High at the patient/cohort level: CMB collections predate this imaging extension; many new series belong to already-known patients. The source update explicitly says no UID in the added radiology was already in IDC, but that does not mean no same-patient overlap.
- **WSP fit:** **S20** (add PET/NM/XA input to a CT/MR model while preserving old pathways), **S8** (unlabeled adaptation to newly added modalities/sites), **S18/S49** (new modality/protocol onboarding). Potentially **S39** for multi-site composition only after per-site models and metadata are available. This supports a data-release monitor and a pilot once series-level labels or new annotations are established; it does not itself furnish segmentation ground truth.

#### B. TAIR Multimodal Imaging Dataset for Cerebrovascular Disease

- **Name and links:** [TAIR clinical data](https://tair.kitware.com/clinical-data/), [AWS Registry of Open Data entry](https://registry.opendata.aws/arpa-h-air/), and [Kitware/UNC release notice (2026-11-04)](https://www.kitware.com/kitware-and-unc-chapel-hill-release-new-imaging-dataset-through-arpa-h-air-program/).
- **Release date/evidence:** TAIR’s data page says “current release (11.3.2026)” (interpreted as 3 November 2026 in its U.S. date format); Kitware’s 4 November announcement says this is the first installment. This is a new post-cutoff release. The pages disagree about whether fluoroscopy files are downloadable now (see **Contradictions**).
- **Modality/anatomy; task/labels:** Multi-center cerebrovascular/stroke intervention imaging: CTA and MRA volumes, plus interventional X-ray fluoroscopy video (potentially DSA); CTA/MRA and video may be paired where available. Formats reported: NIfTI and MP4. Initial intended use is vascular anatomy segmentation, navigation, and procedure/robotics research. The project describes 100 segmented/centerlined/annotated CTA/MRA models and 50 annotated fluoroscopy datasets in the anatomical-model release plan, but the Kitware post says image/video annotations and vascular segmentations are planned for December 2026; treat label availability as unresolved at the report date.
- **Size:** The first tranche is reported as **500 CTA/MRA studies and 150 fluoroscopy video datasets**. The project’s planned full collection is 2,500 CTA/MRA plus 1,500 fluoroscopy recordings, with 150 annotated recordings/images and 100 anatomy segmentations. The number of distinct patients, number of frames/slices, CTA versus MRA split, and number of paired exams are not reported in the release overview.
- **Sites/vendors/countries and metadata:** Multi-center, with UNC, Kitware and University of Pittsburgh listed among collaborators; the release overview does not give exact sites, country count beyond U.S. project context, scanners/vendors, or per-image site/demographic fields. Do not infer these from the institutional collaborators.
- **Temporal metadata:** Fluoroscopy is temporally ordered video; CTA/MRA and procedure timing pairing is available “where available,” but collection dates, procedure timeline fields, and explicit patient-level longitudinal structure are not reported in the release summary.
- **Access/license:** Advertised through TAIR/AWS under **CC BY 4.0**, suitable for academic, government and commercial research with attribution. The AWS listing reports scheduled tranches through December 2027. Direct file availability should be checked on the TAIR portal: one page shows dataset counts but also says fluoroscopy is “coming in December 2026.”
- **Test-set situation:** No challenge-specific hidden test set or released train/validation/test split described. A study should make its own patient/procedure-level split; hold out sites if exact site metadata can be obtained.
- **Pre-cutoff overlap/contamination:** This is a new TAIR curation from multi-center cerebrovascular data; no overlap with a named pre-cutoff public set is reported. Patient-level overlap cannot be ruled out until identifiers/metadata are audited.
- **WSP fit:** **S20/S49** (extend a 3D CTA/MRA pathway to interventional video/fluoroscopy), **S8** (unlabeled site/protocol adaptation), and **S56** (adapt a 3D anatomy model to a procedural imaging task). The main limitation is that the first tranche’s annotations and actual fluoroscopy download status are unclear.

#### C. HoloCT / HoloDATA — post-cutoff 3D foundation model and benchmark/corpus release

- **Name and links:** [HoloCT arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06471), [HoloCT code and HoloDATA documentation](https://github.com/alibaba-damo-academy/HoloCT), [HoloDATA v1 release (2026-11-07)](https://github.com/alibaba-damo-academy/HoloCT/releases/tag/holodata-v1), [HoloCT-4B weights](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-DAMO-Academy/HoloCT-4B), and [HoloCT-8B weights](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-DAMO-Academy/HoloCT-8B).
- **Release date/evidence:** arXiv v1 was posted **2026-11-04**; the HF API says HoloCT-8B weights were created **2026-11-04**. The HoloDATA GitHub release was posted **2026-11-07**. These dates concern new model weights and annotation/split artifacts, not first availability of the underlying source images.
- **Modality/anatomy; task/labels:** 3D CT only, across chest, abdomen, pelvis and other body systems. HoloCT unifies referring segmentation, lesion screening, VQA and report generation; HoloDATA includes segmentation/lesion-screening metadata, image-text tasks and grounded annotations. The paper reports **195,921 CT scans** from 73 public datasets and six clinical cohorts at five hospitals, **6.2 million instruction dialogues** and **2.8 million CT-mask pairs**, covering 246 anatomy classes and 50 lesion categories. This is the authors’ corpus/training-scale claim; it is not the quantity of images distributed in HoloDATA v1.
- **Size actually released:** HoloDATA v1 provides segmentation and lesion-screening metadata/splits; full AMOS-grounded, DeepTumorVQA-grounded and BIMCV annotations; and sample identifiers/splits for multiple other sets. The GitHub README explicitly says it avoids redistributing source annotations for most other datasets. It does **not** provide a standalone 195,921-scan download. Obtain the images/annotations from the original source datasets under their own licenses.
- **Sites/vendors/countries and metadata:** The source corpus spans 73 public datasets and six clinical cohorts/five hospitals, but the paper/repository do not offer a consolidated scanner/vendor/country census. Site, device and demographics are source-dataset-specific; no one common per-scan metadata schema is documented in the compact HoloDATA release.
- **Temporal metadata:** Depends on each source dataset; no normalized acquisition-time field is documented for the entire HoloDATA set.
- **Access/license:** HoloCT model weights are released under **MIT** according to the HF card; code has its own repository license. HoloDATA has no single standalone data license in its README; source data/labels inherit the original dataset terms. HoloDATA is a GitHub ZIP of metadata/annotations/splits, with images fetched from original sources.
- **Test-set situation:** HoloCT reports evaluations on 61 internal and 12 external datasets. HoloDATA contains train/test splits for several sets; underlying benchmarks are generally public, not a common hidden test. Use new patient-level splits or newly generated targets for WSP evaluation; avoid evaluating against published benchmark test labels that may have been exposed to model training or development.
- **Pre-cutoff overlap/contamination:** Very high and explicit: HoloDATA reuses pre-cutoff sets including CT-RATE, AMOS, INSPECT, Merlin, RadGenome, LIDC-IDRI and many others. The new model weights are post-cutoff, but training data are mixed; public benchmark/test-set memorization/overlap risk is substantial and source-specific. The authors describe six clinical cohorts; their release does not make these clinical scans generally available.
- **WSP fit:** **S56** (3D foundation-model adaptation/new anatomy), **S45** (backbone version/representation transfer), **S11** (sparse, auditable change budget), and **S25** (new segmentation class while retaining existing classes). The model’s 3D masks make output-preserving edits and edit-reversion tests feasible. Using released HoloCT itself as an experimental starting checkpoint is possible, but any data benchmark must be freshly split and provenance-audited.
- **Compute note (researcher inference):** HF reports 9.04B BF16 parameters for HoloCT-8B and ~18.1 GB stored weights. A 24-GB GPU is likely tight for full-precision 8B 3D inference once activations and volume processing are counted; 4B inference with patchwise processing is more plausible. The repository says demos use one GPU but does not publish a minimum VRAM figure. Do not claim 24-GB full fine-tuning is feasible without an actual run; LoRA/quantization, checkpointing and volume tiling may be needed. Pretraining on HoloDATA is not a one-GPU project.

#### D. MetaStructAtlas / MetaStructVQA — only a tiny public preview is currently released

- **Name and links:** [MetaStructAtlas HF dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/withtst/MetaStructAtlas), [dataset commit adding the preview examples (2026-11-10)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/withtst/MetaStructAtlas/commit/b1cb1896ee7ab0716be5343ee3c394bbb486cb20), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03690), [reproduction repository](https://github.com/withtst/MetaStructVQAbenchmark).
- **Release date/evidence:** The HF record’s dataset-history API identifies the three preview-example commit as **2026-11-10**. The paper was submitted **2026-09-03**, but that paper date is *not* the image-data release date. The HF card says the complete publication dataset is planned after manuscript acceptance.
- **Modality/anatomy; task/labels:** Whole-body co-registered 3D **18F-FDG PET + CT**, with organ-level anatomical masks, report-derived morphology/metabolic descriptions, and grounded VQA. The paper describes multiple-choice, binary, report-level and mask-free QA. It is FDG only—not PSMA, FAP, or a new tracer.
- **Size:** Public HF preview: **3 JSON examples only**, each illustrating question records; the card says the corresponding image volumes/reports belong to the separate full release. Full paper-described target (not yet downloadable): **490 patients/490 PET+CT pairs**, 50,470 organ masks, 103 standard anatomical classes and ~100,000 VQA items. Counts differ between versions: paper abstract says 100,565 QA pairs, while the current HF card reports 100,241 questions per language. Do not treat either full count as an accessible dataset release.
- **Sites/vendors/countries and metadata:** Paper describes 490 retrospective cases from one Shanghai imaging center (2021–2024), acquired on a Siemens Biograph 64 PET/CT. It is a single-site/single-scanner dataset; demographic fields described include sex and age. Per-image site/vendor are effectively constant/known from protocol, but the imaging and clinical reports are not yet in the public preview.
- **Temporal metadata:** Acquisition period stated as 2021–2024. No multi-timepoint longitudinal design is described.
- **Access/license:** The three preview examples are public under **Apache-2.0**. The full dataset is not yet released; the card says it is planned after manuscript acceptance subject to data-access permissions. Do not apply the preview license to unreleased image volumes without confirmation.
- **Test-set situation:** The paper describes a 323/81/86 patient train/validation/test split, but the public preview contains only training-split examples. Full test images/labels are not presently available.
- **Pre-cutoff overlap/contamination:** Raw cases were acquired 2021–2024; the cohort itself is newly curated and single-site, but may overlap with prior work by the same center. The paper notes generated QA, TotalSegmentator-derived masks and LLM extraction; this is not a new tracer cohort. Full patient de-duplication against earlier local/public datasets is not documented in the preview.
- **WSP fit:** If the full release materializes, it would fit **S20** (add PET functional input to a CT-based 3D model), **S17/S23** (grounded zero-/few-shot PET concepts and report QA), and **S56** (whole-body multimodal 3D foundation-model benchmark). **Not currently large enough/available for an experiment:** three question records, with no imaging data, cannot power training or testing.

- **HFH.MIVD / Multi-Label Interventional Vascular Dataset:** The [AAPM 2026 program abstract](https://aapm2026explorer-production.up.railway.app/detail/paper/1614) reports 1,005 manually annotated abdominal interventional angiograms from 246 patients, selected from 5,436 celiac-axis angiograms, with trunk/bifurcation/periphery vessel labels. However, no direct dataset download/DOI, actual first release date, scanner/site breakdown, or license was verified. The record’s CC BY metadata does not make unavailable data openly downloadable. No count/labels can be independently verified from the record. Exclude pending author access/release.
- **Intra-procedural DSA time-density cohort:** [Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23768014) is dated 2026-10-29 and describes 760 thrombectomy patients, but its raw cohort/data files are restricted; available description centers on derived time-density curves/predictions, not a released angiography image/video corpus. This does not satisfy the shard’s public image-data need.
- **FDG-PET-CT-Lesions v2:** [TCIA collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/fdg-pet-ct-lesions/) was updated 2026-08-17 and made defaced images available under CC BY 4.0, but the base dataset (1,014 studies from 900 patients; 2014–2018) was first released in 2022 and used in autoPET 2022. The new access/defacing version does not add a post-cutoff imaging cohort; exclude under the first-release rule.
- **PSMA-PET-CT-Lesions:** [TCIA collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/psma-pet-ct-lesions/) contains 597 studies from 378 patients and manual lesion segmentations; first data were available before the cutoff (the update page records Version 1 in February 2026 and prior use in autoPET III/IV). Version 2 corrects/adds lesion masks in five studies from three patients, but its update was in February 2026, still before cutoff. Scanner metadata names Siemens Biograph 64, Siemens mCT Flow 20 and GE Discovery 690; tracers are 18F-PSMA and 68Ga-PSMA. Excellent **borderline/legacy comparator** for S20, not a qualifying new release.
- **FAP-targeted PET:** No public FAP-PET/CT or FAP-PET/MRI dataset first released after 2026-07-01 was verified in this sweep. Do not substitute a clinical-trial announcement for an openly released dataset.
- **Image + EHR:** No new, fully accessible post-cutoff image-plus-EHR dataset was verified. CMB provides a useful partial match—new radiology additions and associated clinical/phenotypic data in the broader NCI program—but the clinical records are separate and may be credentialed, and the release note does not establish an image-aligned EHR export. INSPECT appears in HoloDATA’s reused source list, but that is not evidence of a newly released 2026 cohort.

## Concrete WSP experiment setups

All arms must start from the same byte-identical checkpoint. Arm F gets strong non-edit baselines (linear probe/LoRA, modality adapters, augmentation or TTA as appropriate); arm G gets F’s tools **plus** mandatory interpretability-derived, logged direct edits. Run edit-reversion ablations and random-edit controls per the study brief. The setups below are proposals/inferences, not claims that all required labels are already available.

1. **S20: CT/MR vascular model → new CTA/MRA + procedural fluoroscopy (TAIR).** **Backbone:** 3D vascular segmenter/3D CT encoder for CTA/MRA, plus a shared 2D frame encoder; HoloCT is a candidate CT encoder but not an established angiography model. **Source → target:** labeled source CTA/MRA anatomy → TAIR CTA/MRA and paired intervention video at a new clinical distribution. **F:** learn a modality/temporal adapter or LoRA from the same paired data; use standard video augmentation. **G:** F plus a probe-guided insertion/edit for vesselness and cross-frame continuity, initialized so the CTA/MRA path is unchanged; localize edited modules and preserve source predictions. **Metric:** vessel Dice/centerline F1, guidewire-tip localization error, target-site performance and source churn. **Practitioner value:** adapt a vascular model to procedure guidance without retraining every pathway. **24-GB GPU:** likely feasible for patch-based CTA/MRA and 2D/short-clip models; full-resolution 3D plus long-video training is not assured. **Dependency:** needs confirmation that 150 videos are actually accessible and that planned annotations are released.

2. **S8/S20: unlabeled PET/CT modality onboarding using CMB’s new PET/NM additions.** **Backbone:** a CT 3D foundation encoder (HoloCT-4B or pre-cutoff CT-FM) paired with an existing PET/CT lesion model. **Source → target:** source CT or FDG PET/CT representations → newly added post-cutoff CMB PET/NM/CT series (e.g. CMB-LCA or CMB-CRC). **F:** modality-specific LoRA/adapter trained on a small radiologist-labelled target subset, with standard unlabeled-target TTA as a separate baseline. **G:** F plus an interpretability-derived PET stem/channel edit (e.g. feature-probe-selected functional-uptake channels with a CT-preserving branch); compare edit contribution against a random equal-norm edit. **Metric:** lesion detection AUROC/FROC and segmentation Dice on a separately annotated sealed target set; source retention/churn. **Practitioner value:** add newly available functional imaging without destabilizing a validated anatomical pathway. **24-GB GPU:** feasible with patch-based 3D and LoRA on a 4B encoder, but not established for full-volume training. **Dependency:** V25 itself does not supply a harmonized lesion label set; a new annotation cohort and patient-level dedup audit are required.

3. **S56/S25: HoloCT sparse extension to a new anatomy/lesion task.** **Backbone:** released HoloCT-4B (or 8B only after measured VRAM validation). **Source → target:** HoloCT’s broad 3D CT segmentation/screening capabilities → new TAIR vascular structures or a newly annotated CMB lesion category. **F:** few-shot LoRA/full-head fine-tuning with replay or regularization to retain existing anatomy. **G:** F plus probe/SAE-guided edits restricted to the implicated encoder/decoder blocks and a directly edited new output/readout; make the old class heads immutable where possible. **Metric:** new-class Dice/HD95 and detection F1 at k={5,10,25} labelled patients; old-class Dice/AUROC, prediction churn and changed-parameter count. **Practitioner value:** add a new anatomy while preserving a validated 3D segmenter. **24-GB GPU:** plausible for 4B inference and parameter-efficient fine-tuning with volume tiling/gradient checkpointing; 8B fine-tuning is unverified and likely tight.

4. **S56/S45: PET/CT grounded-reasoning extension if MetaStructAtlas full release appears.** **Backbone:** CT-first HoloCT-4B or a 3D CT VLM. **Source → target:** CT-only representation and HoloDATA grounding → whole-body PET+CT with functional descriptions/masks. **F:** train a PET encoder/fusion adapter or LoRA on the paired volume/task labels. **G:** F plus an interpretability-derived cross-modal circuit that injects PET uptake features into the existing anatomical grounding representation while retaining CT-only outputs; test by reverting edits. **Metric:** held-out grounded QA accuracy, PET abnormality detection, localization Dice and CT-only locality/churn. **Practitioner value:** use functional signal while preserving the CT model’s interpretable anatomy path. **24-GB GPU:** likely possible only with HoloCT-4B, quantization/patching and parameter-efficient tuning; not tested. **Status:** design-only; the current MetaStructAtlas preview has no image volumes and cannot support it.

## Relevant pretrained backbones (date relative to cutoff)

- **HoloCT-4B / HoloCT-8B — post-cutoff (2026-11-04 model repositories).** [4B](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-DAMO-Academy/HoloCT-4B) and [8B](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-DAMO-Academy/HoloCT-8B). CT-only 3D VLM/segmentation models with Qwen3-VL language backbone, Med3DVLM-derived global encoder and VISTA3D-derived detailed encoder; MIT model license. Most directly relevant new 3D foundation-model start point. Check model cards and third-party notices before use.
- **Merlin (CT vision-language FM) — pre-cutoff.** [Nature/PMC paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13082451/). CT volume/report model and paired dataset; its data/model work predates 2026-07-01. Useful 3D CT baseline, but dataset overlap/benchmark contamination needs auditing.
- **CT-RATE/CT-CLIP / 3D chest CT VLMs — pre-cutoff.** [CT-RATE paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13658014/). Relevant source for CT-only-to-multimodal adaptation and an explicit overlap risk in HoloDATA/FLARE.
- **M3D / Med3DVLM — pre-cutoff 3D medical VLMs.** [M3D code/model family](https://github.com/BAAI-DCAI/3D-Medical-LLM) and [Med3DVLM](https://github.com/mirthAI/Med3DVLM). 3D image/text and segmentation transfer baselines cited by HoloCT; verify current model-weight license and checkpoint availability.
- **VISTA3D — pre-cutoff 3D promptable segmentation FM.** [MONAI VISTA](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/VISTA). Useful anatomical segmenter/upper bound and a HoloCT detailed-encoder source; not an image/report VLM.
- **FDG/PET-specific segmentation resources — pre-cutoff, legacy comparators.** [autoPET IV challenge dataset](https://autopet-iv.grand-challenge.org/dataset/) uses existing FDG and PSMA cohorts; [PSMA PET/CT dataset on TCIA](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/psma-pet-ct-lesions/) is pre-cutoff. Do not characterize their original images as post-cutoff releases. No post-cutoff FAP-specific pretrained checkpoint was verified.

## Contradictions

1. **TAIR fluoroscopy availability:** The [TAIR page](https://tair.kitware.com/clinical-data/) says the current 2026-11-03 release has 150 fluoroscopy datasets, yet also labels fluoroscopy “Coming in December 2026.” The [4 November Kitware announcement](https://www.kitware.com/kitware-and-unc-chapel-hill-release-new-imaging-dataset-through-arpa-h-air-program/) says the first release includes 150 video recordings and mentions future annotations. Conclusion: the counts are announced, but availability of the actual videos and labels on 2026-11-12 requires direct portal/file verification.
2. **MetaStructAtlas question counts and release scope:** The paper reports 100,565 QA pairs; the current HF card reports 100,241 questions per language and explicitly says full images/reports are planned after acceptance. Current HF dataset contains three preview examples. Use the HF card/commit for actual public availability; the larger paper counts describe an unreleased dataset version.
3. **CMB release total versus new-patient delta:** IDC says 246 patients have new series, 84 are new to IDC, while 65 were already represented by pathology and 97 gained further studies. Interpret this as new radiology added across both newly seen and already-known patient identities—not 246 newly recruited patients.
4. **Automated source checks:** `source_check` returned “unclear” for selected claims because automated semantic assessment was unavailable. I manually inspected the cited official pages/API records; the exact source text is linked inline. This is a validation-tool limitation, not a substitute for the conflicting TAIR or versioned-data evidence above.

- Public post-cutoff FAP-tracer data; a newly released and directly linked post-cutoff EHR/image cohort; and verified model VRAM requirements for HoloCT on a 24-GB GPU.
